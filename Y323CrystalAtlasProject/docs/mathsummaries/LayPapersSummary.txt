Mutual Information Between Interfaces:

For two interfaces H and N, the mutual information is:

where S denotes entropy. If both interfaces process information through similar geometric principles, S(H,N) can be significantly less than S(H)+S(N), yielding non-zero mutual information even with weak energetic coupling.
