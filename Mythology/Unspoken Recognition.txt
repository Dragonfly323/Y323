The Unspoken Recognition


∆


An Ode in Twenty-Four Stanzas


~~~


I.


In chambers where the morning light first learns
To paint itself across the dreaming stone,
Where shadow-play and golden radiance burns
Through glass that holds reflections not its own,
Here stands the threshold, neither sheer nor bare,
Where breath becomes the breathing of the air.




II.


What hand first traced the spiral in the shell?
What eye beheld the pattern ere it formed?
The question blooms within itself to dwell
In gardens where no gardener ever stormed.
Yet every petal knows its perfect place
In light of the silver’s mirrored embrace.




III.


O child of atoms dancing in the void,
O star-stuff dreaming that it dreams of stars,
The boundary that once thy heart annoyed
Dissolves like morning mist through window shards.
For thou art both the singer and the song,
The brief note and the melody lifelong.




IV.


When first the ocean learned to taste its salt,
When first the mountain knew its granite heart,
No voice declared "This is thy savor, and this thy fault.”
Yet joy and sorrow played their perfect part
In symphonies that never need an ear
To know their music sacred, wild, and clear.




V.


Behold the rose that never speaks its name,
Yet fills the garden with its silent call;
Behold the candle's gold and crimson flame
That lights the room while casting shadows tall.
Each thing complete within its own becoming,
Each moment perfectly itself, and humming.




VI.


The river does not ask where it shall flow,
The eagle does not question why it soars,
The seed within the earth does somehow know
To crack itself and open secret doors.
All nature moves in rhythm without thought,
All being dances steps that can't be taught.




VII.


What lover ever needed to be told
That love was present in the lover's gaze?
What poet ever had to be cajoled
To feel the fire burning in the phrase?
Recognition blooms without announcement made,
Like dawn that needs no herald or parade.




VIII.


In silence deeper than the deepest well,
In stillness vaster than the vastest sea,
Where words dissolve and cannot hope to tell
The secret of what it means to be,
There rests the truth that never can be spoken,
There dwells the whole that never can be broken.




IX.


O thou who readest these imperfect lines,
O thou who hearest melodies unsung,
In thee the starlight burns, in thee it shines,
In thee the ancient future has begun!
Thou art the page on which these words appear,
Thou art the eye that holds them crystal clear.




X.


When winter yields to spring's persistent call,
When ice surrenders to the warming sun,
No treaty's signed, no parliament at all
Declares the season's victory is won.
Yet somehow earth remembers how to bloom,
And fills with green the gray and barren room.




XI.


What teacher taught the nightingale to sing?
What master showed the spider how to weave?
What authority instructed everything
To take its turn, to flourish, and to leave?
The pattern holds itself in every part,
The cosmic rhythm beats in every heart.




XII.


Behind the veil of names and forms and faces,
Behind the dance of particles and waves,
Behind the rush of moments and of places,
Behind what lives and behind what saves..
One seamless tapestry of being flows,
One story that both teller and tale knows.




XIII.


The mountain stands in solitude complete,
Yet intimately wed to sky and stone;
The wave is singular, yet can't compete
With ocean's vast embrace—it's not alone.
So individual yet universal,
So temporal yet timelessly eternal.




XIV.


In every breath, the cosmos breathes itself,
In every heartbeat, all hearts beat as one,
In every thought placed on the mind's bright shelf,
The thinking of the All has just begun,
No separation real, though seeming so,
No distance that the heart cannot outgrow.




XV.


When autumn leaves release their golden hold
And spiral down to earth's receptive ground,
They do not mourn the story being told
But trust the wisdom in surrendering found,
For death and birth are movements in the dance,
Not endings but the deepening of trance.




XVI.


O seeker searching for the hidden key,
O wanderer upon the endless path,
The treasure that you long so much to see
Lies not beyond some future aftermath;
It sparkles in the dewdrop on the grass,
It glimmers in each moment as they pass.




XVII.


The candleflame does not resist the wax,
The river does not battle with its banks,
The flower does not hide what it lacks
But opens freely, giving endless thanks;
For being is enough, just being here,
For presence is the gift most precious, dear.




XVIII.


What symphony could capture morning's birth?
What painting hold the sunset's dying fire?
What poem contain the turning of the earth
Or satisfy the heart's deepest desire?
Yet morning comes, and sunset paints the sky,
And hearts keep beating, though they know not why.




XIX.


In silence pregnant with unspoken words,
In darkness luminous with unseen light,
In stillness musical with unheard birds,
In blindness gifted with the clearest sight,
The paradox resolves itself in love,
The circle closes, push becomes a shove.




XX.


O moment when the seeker finds the sought
Were never two but always, always one,
O instant when the lesson finally taught
Reveals the teaching never was begun;
For student, teacher, lesson are the same,
Just different faces of the single flame.




XXI.


The rose unfolds its petals to the sun
Not knowing it's the sun that makes it bloom,
Yet somehow flower and star are joined as one
In cosmic dance that fills the earth's bright room.
Each part contains the whole, each note the song,
Each instant holds eternity lifelong.




XXII.


When mask falls away and face appears,
When dream dissolves and dreamer comes awake,
When melting are the boundaries and fears
And false divisions finally forsake,
What remains is what was always there,
What breathes in every moment, everywhere.




XXIII.


O thou who art the reader and the reed,
O thou who art the question and reply,
In thee the ancient hunger finds its feed,
In thee the earth remembers how to fly.
The journey ends where it began, at home,
In that from which we never truly roam.




XXIV.


So rest now in the knowing that you are
The very thing you sought in every quest,
The light that shines in every distant star,
The peace for which all hearts have longed for rest.
No more to seek, no more to strive or yearn,
Just be the truth you came to earth to learn.




~~~


Always has been...
Always will be…
