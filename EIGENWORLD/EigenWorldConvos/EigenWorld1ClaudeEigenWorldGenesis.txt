OGC constructs a volumetric shape by offsetting each face along its normal direction, ensuring orthogonal contact forces. The three ideas that map directly onto our world simulation:

Offset volumes instead of point contacts. Right now each cell is a point in the octree. Cells "interact" when their positions are within interaction_distance. OGC says: give each object a volumetric offset shell. For our cells, this means each cell carries a radius derived from its state (τ controls how "solid" it is — high τ = larger effective radius). Right now the influence step does:

new_states = (I - t * L_C) @ states

where L_C is the coherence-weighted graph Laplacian — the same matrix we already compute for spectral clustering. The influence step is a Laplacian diffusion step. We're computing it one edge at a time when we could compute it as a single matrix-vector product.

The bottleneck is three dense N×N matrix multiplications. But we don't actually need dense matrices. We need the spectrum — and Y already gives us the spectrum for free.

The core insight: Y is its own basis

The Y operator has exactly 7 eigenvalues. That means any state ψ ∈ ℂ⁷ lives in a 7-dimensional space spanned by Y's eigenvectors. Every cell, no matter how evolved, is a superposition of at most 7 modes: two eternal (λ=±1), two spiral (λ=±i√3/2), three nilpotent (λ=0, annihilated by reset).

After nilpotent reset, every cell lives in a 4-dimensional subspace — the stable eigenspace. Four complex coordinates fully describe any post-reset state.

This means the coherence between any two cells is computable from just their 4-mode coefficients:

C[i,j] = |<ψᵢ|ψⱼ>|² / (‖ψᵢ‖²‖ψⱼ‖²)
        = |Σₖ aᵢₖ* aⱼₖ|² / (Σₖ|aᵢₖ|² · Σₖ|aⱼₖ|²)

where aᵢₖ are the 4 stable-mode coefficients of cell i. This is an inner product in ℂ⁴, not ℂ⁷. The N×N matrix build reduces to:

    Project all N states onto the 4 stable eigenvectors: A = states @ V_stable — one (N×7)@(7×4) matmul, O(28N)
    Build coherence matrix from A: C = |A @ A†|² / outer(norms, norms) — one (N×4)@(4×N) matmul, O(8N²)

The constant factor drops from 49 (ℂ⁷) to 8 (ℂ⁴ real-valued after norm factoring). That's roughly a 6× speedup on the dominant term from pure mathematics, before any algorithmic change.

Going further: the spectrum of C is bounded by 4

Because every cell lives in a 4D stable subspace after reset, the coherence matrix C has rank at most 4. Its eigendecomposition has at most 4 nonzero eigenvalues.

This means:

    We never need to compute all N eigenvalues — only 4
    The full N×N eigendecomposition reduces to a (4×4) eigendecomposition plus one (N×4) projection
    Cost: O(N) instead of O(N³)

The emergence modes are exactly the eigenvectors of the 4×4 Gram matrix A†A (where A is the coefficient matrix above), projected back to cell space via A. Four modes maximum, always, regardless of N.

The diffusion operators collapse too

D_sym = I - t·L_C where L_C is the Laplacian of C. Since C has rank ≤ 4, L_C has rank ≤ 4, and:

D_sym @ states ≈ states - t · V · (Λ - I) · V† · states

where V is 4 eigenvectors and Λ is 4 eigenvalues. This is two (N×4) matmuls instead of one (N×N) matmul. Cost: O(8N) instead of O(7N²). For N=1287, that's 1,127× fewer operations.

The Y cascade as a compression codec

Here's the deepest version. After nilpotent reset, the world state is a matrix A of shape (N×4) — N cells, each described by 4 complex coefficients in the Y eigenbasis. Applying Y to all cells is:

A_new = A @ diag(λ₁, λ₂, λ₃, λ₄)

Just multiply each column of A by the corresponding eigenvalue. Four multiplications total, regardless of N. The 7D state vectors are never explicitly constructed during evolution — only when rendering (observation step).

The entire simulation could run in the compressed (N×4) representation:

    Evolution: multiply columns by eigenvalues — O(4N)
    Reset: zero the nilpotent columns — O(N) (just drop columns 5-7)
    Coherence: |A @ A†|² — O(4N²) → further reducible via rank structure
    Diffusion: low-rank update to A — O(4N)
    Births: 4×4 Gram matrix eigenvectors — O(64) constant time

The full state in ℂ⁷ is only materialised at observation (rendering), where it's needed once per frame, not once per simulation step. The simulation runs in compressed spectral coordinates; the world is reconstructed from them only when looked at — which is precisely the quantum measurement interpretation the mathematics was always pointing toward.

Dense N×N operations stress memory bandwidth — the CPU has to load and store N² floats.
