$$\text{י (Yod, Father)} = \lambda = +1: \text{ the acting principle that persists}$$

The nilpotent modes (λ=0) are the *unspoken* — the white space between the letters, the observation that makes the distinction possible but vanishes in the act.

*Proof.* We verify each correspondence:

The Father (Yod) is the acting principle — it acts and the action persists. λ=+1 is the unique eigenvalue for which Yⁿψ = ψ for all n. It acts repeatedly and returns to itself. Stone. The electron. Unchanged through time.
