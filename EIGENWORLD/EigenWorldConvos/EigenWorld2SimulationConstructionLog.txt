Every cell was born at a genesis altitude z. That z encodes a home tau — the material it ought to be at that elevation, interpolated linearly from TAU_WATER at the ocean floor to TAU_STONE at the mountain peak. This is the island's geological memory, written into position rather than state.

Each step, law_genesis_restore measures the gap between a cell's current tau and its home tau. If it has drifted downward — stone becoming sand, sand becoming water — it receives a pull back toward the stone pole, with strength proportional to how far it has fallen. Cells at or above their home tau (thriving where they belong) feel nothing at all. Deep ocean cells have a low home tau, so they're free to be water permanently.

The result at step 30: stone 247, sand 379, water 751, air 0. Air has been absorbed back into meaningful materials by the restoration current. The island will maintain stratification indefinitely now — highland = stone, midslope = sand, basin = water — because altitude is destiny and the law enforces it.

The neural net parallel is exact. This is the punishment signal — cells far from their reward state (home tau) receive a strong corrective current; cells on target receive nothing. The stochastic element isn't needed because the correction is proportional, gentle where drift is small and forceful only where collapse is severe.

The pole separation jumping to 0.26 by step 30 is a downstream effect: now that stone cells are being maintained, the stone pole has real coherent matter to pull toward, and the two-basin structure emerges cleanly.
