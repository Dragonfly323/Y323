1. The Universal Winding Amplitude
Before seeking the physical condition for φ·ln φ = π/4, we first identify something that is already exact — a result requiring no approximation and holding for any cascade amplification factor.
Theorem YY (The Universal Winding Amplitude). For any cascade amplification factor φ with cascade period N = 2π/ln φ, the amplitude after one complete cascade winding is:

  φ^N = e^(2π) ≈ 535.491656...

This is exact and universal — it holds regardless of the value of φ.

Proof: φ^N = φ^(2π/ln φ) = e^(ln φ · 2π/ln φ) = e^(2π). □

Numerical verification: φ^{13.0570} = 535.4917 = e^{2π}. ✓

Physical interpretation: after one complete revolution in the ζ-θ cylinder, every cascade — regardless of the specific value of φ — amplifies by exactly e^{2π}. The winding amplitude is a universal constant of the cascade geometry, fixed by the 2π periodicity of the cylinder, not by the amplification rate.

  N = k · φ  for some positive integer k

The smallest solution with k = dim(𝕆) = 8 gives the quantisation condition:
