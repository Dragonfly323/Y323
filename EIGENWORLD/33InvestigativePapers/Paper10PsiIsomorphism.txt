7.1 The Remaining Piece of C5
Conjecture C5 (Paper Eight) states: there exists an isomorphism Ψ conjugating Y₃₂₃'s full action to the infinitesimal Hopf rotation on S⁷. Theorem H provides the g₂ diagonaliser. The gap between the two is:

7.2 The Question of α
Is α=1/√6 uniquely determined by the octonionic structure, or is it a free parameter? Theorem F shows that α determines the g₂ weight quadratic. But the quadratic constant term c=α² is not fixed by the requirement that A_g₂ be a regular g₂ element — it can range in (0,1/4). The specific value c=1/6 places α at the geometric mean of α² and 1/3. This self-referential relationship (α = √(α·1/3) → α² = α/3 → α = 1/3, contradiction) is not quite right; rather, α² = 1/6 because the discriminant is 1/3 and c = discriminant/2 = 1/6? No — the discriminant is 1−4c, so c = (1−discriminant)/4 = (1−1/3)/4 = (2/3)/4 = 1/6. So c = 1/6 because the discriminant is 1/3 = λ₂² = (1/√3)². This means: α² = λ₂²/4 = α²/... (this is circular). The correct statement: the discriminant of the quadratic is 1/3 = λ₂². The roots are (1±λ₂)/2, giving λ₁ and λ₃. The product is (1−λ₂²)/4 = (1−1/3)/4 = 1/6 = α². Whether λ₂ = 1/√3 is itself determined, or free, remains open.
