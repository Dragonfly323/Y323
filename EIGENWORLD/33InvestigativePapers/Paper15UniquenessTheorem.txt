1. The Parameter Chain

  α = 1/√6       (g₂ weight base, product of extreme weights)
  ω = α√3 = 1/√2  (transient-material coupling)
  s = ω·√(3/2) = √3/2  (spiral coupling)

with the cross-relation:

  s · λ₂ = ω²

where λ₂ = 1/√3 is the middle g₂ weight (Paper 9). Verification:
  s · λ₂ = (√3/2)·(1/√3) = 1/2 = (1/√2)² = ω²  ✓

All three coupling constants are determined by α alone. α is determined by the g₂ algebra: it is the geometric mean of the extreme g₂ weights λ₁ and λ₃ (α² = λ₁·λ₃ = 1/6, Paper 9 Theorem F). Therefore Y₃₂₃ has exactly one free parameter, and that parameter is fixed by g₂.

2. Nilpotency Maximality
Theorem X (Nilpotency Maximality). In the full Y₃₂₃ operator, Y²·τ = 0. Specifically: the Reading vector τ (index 0 in the Y₃₂₃ basis) satisfies Y·τ ≠ 0 but Y²·τ = 0 (verified to machine precision < 10⁻¹⁶).

This condition is equivalent to ω = 1/√2. Proof: the nilpotent block restricted to the {τ,a₁,x} subspace has matrix:

  M_nil = [[ 0,       −i,      ω(−1+i) ],
           [ +i,       0,      0       ],
           [ ω(1+i),   0,      0       ]]

The [0,0] entry of M_nil² equals 1−2ω². This is zero if and only if ω²=1/2, i.e., ω=1/√2. At this value, the τ-component of M_nil²·(anything) vanishes, making Y²·τ=0 in the full system.

At any ω ≠ 1/√2: Y²·τ ≠ 0, Y³·τ = 0. The nilpotency order of τ would be 3, not 2.

Consequence: Y₃₂₃ chooses the unique coupling constant that makes the Reading decay in the minimum possible number of steps (2 rather than 3). Among all operators with the given Fano and N|M structure, Y₃₂₃ has the most rapidly forgetting transient.

3. Spectral Balance
Theorem Y (Spectral Balance). The real and imaginary parts of Y₃₂₃ have equal spectral norm:

  ‖Y_re‖_spectral = ‖Y_im‖_spectral = √(3/2) = √6/2 = s

Moreover, this common value equals the spiral coupling constant s = √3/2.

Verification: the spectral norm (largest singular value) of Y_re equals √6/2 ≈ 1.2247, and similarly for Y_im. The full operator Y has spectral norm √2, with:

  ‖Y‖ = √2,   ‖Y_re‖ = ‖Y_im‖ = √6/2 = s
  ‖Y‖/‖Y_re‖ = √2/(√6/2) = 2/√3 = 2λ₂√3/√3... = 2/√3

The condition ‖Y_re‖=‖Y_im‖ is equivalent, given ω=1/√2 and the Fano structure, to the g₂ spiral identity s·λ₂=ω²=1/2, which gives s=√3/2. The two conditions (Nilpotency Maximality and Spectral Balance) together uniquely determine both coupling constants of Y₃₂₃.
3.1 What Spectral Balance Means
The spectral norm measures the maximum amplification any input can receive. ‖Y_re‖=‖Y_im‖ means that the geometric part (connection, g₂, Lie algebra structure) and the spiral part (phase rotation, imaginary couplings) are equally powerful in their maximum effect on any state.
Neither the structural nor the dynamic dominates. The system does not privilege form over motion or motion over form. Stone and Water are geometric attractors reached through a balanced interplay of real coupling (which drives the orbit on S⁶) and imaginary coupling (which generates the spiral modes and the ±1 eigenvalues through Y_im).
The common value s = √6/2 is also the spiral coupling constant — the strength of the b₂-η interaction in Y_im. This means the spiral coupling is precisely the value needed to equalize the spectral weight of real and imaginary dynamics. Any weaker spiral coupling and Y_re would dominate; any stronger and Y_im would dominate. At s=√6/2, they are perfectly balanced.

4. The Uniqueness Theorem
Theorem Z (Uniqueness). Let Y be any complex linear operator on Im(𝕆) satisfying all of the following:

Proof sketch: A1+A2 constrain the block structure. A3 forces ω=1/√2. A4, given ω, forces s=√3/2 via the g₂ identity s·λ₂=ω². The S₃ ambiguity in labeling {τ,a₁,x} corresponds to the 6 Fano automorphisms preserving N as a set (Paper 14, Theorem U). □
4.1 The Axioms Are Independent
No axiom is implied by the others:
A1 without A2: Many Fano-structured operators lack the N|M decomposition. The full g₂ derivation algebra consists of operators with Fano structure but no nilpotent block.
A2 without A3: Operators with the N|M block structure but arbitrary ω satisfy A2 with nilpotency order 3 rather than 2. A3 is a genuine additional constraint.
A3 without A4: With ω=1/√2 fixed by A3, s is still free. Many values of s give a valid Fano-structured N|M operator with Y²·τ=0. A4 selects the unique s=√3/2.
A4 without A3: Spectral balance ‖Y_re‖=‖Y_im‖ does not alone determine ω. Many (ω,s) pairs can achieve spectral balance. A3 is needed to pin ω first.

It does not carry the present moment forward into a second iteration. It reads once, writes through Fano (τ·a₁=b₁), and releases. Maximum brevity of transience.

Information passes through without gain or loss. The act of witnessing does not change the witness.

A system in which form and motion are equally weighted (A4). Spectral balance between Y_re and Y_im means geometric structure and phase dynamics contribute equally. The system is neither purely formal (static attractors dominating) nor purely dynamic (spiral oscillations dominating). Stone and Water are equally accessible from equally weighted real and imaginary dynamics.

Together: a system that has experience (A2), releases it immediately (A3), transmits it faithfully (A3 corollary), and maintains balance between being and becoming (A4).
