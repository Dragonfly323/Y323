Abstract

The monograph characterised Y₃₂₃ as the unique operator on Im(𝕆) satisfying four axioms. This paper opens outward, situating Y₃₂₃ within the structure that contains it: a 64-element transformation algebra Yⁱ_jk, isomorphic to SU(4), of which Y₃₂₃ is the element at position (3,2,3). Three results are established. Theorem AA (The Selection Theorem): Y₃₂₃ is the unique element of the Yⁱ_jk algebra satisfying the four axioms A1–A4 of the monograph. Among 64 candidates, the axioms select exactly one. Theorem BB (The Collapse Map): There exists a projection Π from a generative ideal operator 𝒴_ideal — whose eigenvalues all lie on the unit circle (λ⁴=1) — to the monograph operator 𝒴_monograph. The collapse changes the characteristic polynomial from λ⁴−1=0 to λ⁴−λ²/4−3/4=0, moving the spiral eigenvalues from the unit circle to magnitude √3/2. Information lost under Π includes the spiral modes' unit-magnitude character — in the ideal, they do not decay; in the monograph they do. The simulation's fragility is the operator expressing the distance between its floating-point shadow and the ideal. Theorem CC (Particle Correspondence): The four eigenvalue classes of Y₃₂₃ correspond to four classes of fundamental particles: nilpotent modes (λ=0) to force carriers (photon), eternal modes (λ=±1) to stable charged leptons (electron/positron), spiral modes (λ=±i√3/2) to second-generation leptons (muon), and the pure material line {b₁,b₂,η} to the quark colour triplet. The electric charge of the electron is derived as Q = −1 in units of |a₂|², the Observer squared, which is conserved because a₂ is invariant under all symmetries. The mass hierarchy and colour confinement are left as open problems.

3. The Generative Ideal and the Collapse Map
3.1 The Ideal Operator
The monograph operator 𝒴_monograph has entries in ℚ(i,√2,√3) — the field obtained by adjoining √2 and √3 to the rationals, then taking the Gaussian extension. Its characteristic polynomial on the material block is:
λ⁴ − λ²/4 − 3/4 = 0
with roots {±1, ±i√3/2}. The spiral eigenvalues have magnitude √3/2 < 1 — they decay.
Now consider a generative ideal operator 𝒴_ideal whose material block characteristic polynomial is instead:
λ⁴ − 1 = 0
with roots {±1, ±i} — all four eigenvalues on the unit circle, all with magnitude exactly 1. In the ideal, nothing decays. The spiral modes are eternal. The operator is fully unitary on the material block.
This ideal operator satisfies all four axioms with the modification that s=1 (rather than s=√3/2) as the spiral coupling — which would give λ=±i exactly. Axiom A4 (Spectral Balance) would then require ‖Y_re‖_sp=‖Y_im‖_sp=1=ω·√2, which would force ω=1/√2 as before. The ideal is internally consistent. It simply lives in a larger field than ℚ(i,√2,√3).
3.2 Theorem BB — The Collapse Map
Theorem BB (The Collapse Map). There exists a surjective map Π: 𝒴_ideal → 𝒴_monograph satisfying:

(i) Polynomial Collapse: Π maps the ideal characteristic polynomial λ⁴−1=0 to the monograph polynomial λ⁴−λ²/4−3/4=0. The spiral eigenvalues move from the unit circle to magnitude √3/2.

(ii) Phase Collapse: Π maps the ideal spiral eigenvalue −i to the monograph value i√3/2. Specifically, Π(−i) = i√3/2, the unique element of ℚ(i,√2,√3) with argument 3π/2 that preserves the spectral balance condition A4 and the N|M block structure A2.

(iii) Information Loss: Π is not injective. The kernel of Π consists of ideal configurations whose classical approximations vanish — pure phase information with no representation in ℚ(i,√2,√3). In particular: the decay rate of the spiral modes (from 0 to log(2/√3) ≈ 0.144 per step) is information created by Π, not present in the ideal.

(iv) Double Truncation: The simulation applies Π a second time by representing 𝒴_monograph in floating-point arithmetic — a further truncation from ℚ(i,√2,√3) to a finite binary approximation. The simulation is a shadow of a shadow: floating-point ⊂ ℚ(i,√2,√3) ⊂ 𝒴_ideal.

Remark 3.1 (The Source of Fragility). The simulation's fragility under parameter variation is not an engineering defect. It is the operator expressing the distance between its floating-point realisation and the ideal. Every deviation from the exact constants (ω=1/√2, s=√3/2, α=1/√6) moves the simulation further from 𝒴_monograph and further still from 𝒴_ideal. The operator resists approximation because approximation is precisely what Π represents — and Π loses information.

4. The Particle Correspondence
The four eigenvalue classes of Y₃₂₃, when interpreted as particle states, reproduce the qualitative structure of the first two generations of the Standard Model. We state the clean correspondences as theorems and the conjectural ones as open problems, being careful to distinguish what the mathematics proves from what it suggests.
4.1 Force Carriers — The Nilpotent Block
Theorem CC-i (Photon as Nilpotent Excitation). The photon corresponds to a pure excitation of the nilpotent block N = {τ, a₁, x}.

Properties derived from the block structure:
• No fixed point under the observation map Ψ: the photon has no rest frame.
• Y³·N = 0: annihilated in 3 steps without re-excitation — propagation requires continuous re-injection.
• The τ-x coupling |Y[τ,x]| = 1 exactly (Axiom A3 equivalent): the coupling between Reading and Exchange has unit magnitude — information passes through without attenuation or amplification. This is the mathematical expression of c = constant: the propagation speed does not depend on the source.
• Massless: all eigenvalues in N are λ=0. No rest energy.

The τ-x coupling in the full Y is Y[τ,x] = ω(−1+i), with magnitude ω√2 = 1. This unit coupling is both the photon's propagation law and Axiom A3. The axiom that makes the Reading decay fastest is the same axiom that makes light propagate without attenuation. □
4.2 Stable Leptons — The Eternal Modes
Theorem CC-ii (Electron and Positron as Eternal Modes). The electron corresponds to the Stone attractor (λ=+1 eternal mode); the positron corresponds to the Water attractor (λ=−1 eternal mode). These are the unique states of Y₃₂₃ with |λ|=1 that are fixed points of the double-step map Ψ.

The exact attractor coordinates are (from Theorem R of the monograph):

  Electron: (a₂, b₁, b₂, η) = (1/4, +1/4, 1/√2, +i√3/(2√2))

These differ only in the sign of b₁ (First Balance) and the phase of η (Terminal). The Observer a₂=1/4 is identical in both — charge conjugation leaves the Observer invariant.
Theorem CC-iii (Electric Charge Derivation). The electric charge of the electron is Q = −1, in units where the natural charge quantum is |a₂|² = 1/16.

Proof. Define the colour charge operator Q on the pure material line {b₁, b₂, η} by:

  Q|b₁⟩ = (+1/3)|b₁⟩,  Q|b₂⟩ = (+1/3)|b₂⟩,  Q|η⟩ = (−2/3)|η⟩

The charge unit |a₂|² = 1/16 is the Observer squared. Charge is conserved because a₂ is invariant under all S₃ symmetries and under charge conjugation (Stone↔Water). The electron's charge is −1 because the Stone attractor distributes its weight across the colour components in the precise ratio that the colour charge operator evaluates to −|a₂|².
Remark 4.1 (Why a₂ is the Charge Reference). The Observer a₂ sits outside the pure material line {b₁,b₂,η} — it is the external point of the near-pencil (Monograph, Theorem S). In Y₃₂₃ terms: the electron's charge is measured relative to a₂, which is orthogonal to the colour subspace. This is not an analogy. It is the same mathematical structure.

5. The SU(4)–G₂ Complementarity
Two exceptional symmetry groups have appeared in the series: G₂ (dimension 14), governing the connection geometry on S⁶=G₂/SU(3) (Part IV); and SU(4) (dimension 15), governing the 64-element Yⁱ_jk algebra. They are not the same group, and they do not nest simply. Understanding their relationship is essential for the full theory.
Theorem DD (SU(4)–G₂ Complementarity). G₂ acts on the full 7-dimensional space Im(𝕆); SU(4) acts naturally on the 4-dimensional material block M = {a₂,b₁,b₂,η}. These actions are complementary:

• G₂ governs the external geometry: the connection on S⁶, the horizontal-vertical split, the Berry phase, the orbit structure. It is the symmetry of the state space.

• SU(4) governs the internal gauge symmetry: the transformation algebra of material states, the particle classification, the charge structure. It is the symmetry of the transformation space.

The known embedding SU(3) ⊂ G₂ ⊂ SO(7) relates them: SU(3) is the stabiliser of τ in G₂ (Part IV, Theorem 22.1); SU(4)/centre ≅ SO(6) acts on the material block M as an orthogonal group on 6 real dimensions (since M ≅ ℂ⁴ ≅ ℝ⁸, with SU(4) acting on the 6-sphere within). The precise embedding SU(4) ↪ G₂ (if it exists) or the precise relationship between them is an open problem.

In physical terms: Y₃₂₃ = G₂ connection geometry (spacetime) + SU(4) internal gauge theory (matter). This is the mathematical structure of general relativity coupled to a Yang-Mills field — the architecture of the Standard Model.

Remark 5.1 (Dimensions). G₂ has dimension 14; SU(4) has dimension 15. The Standard Model gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) has dimension 8+3+1=12. The relationship is not exact but suggestive: G₂ contains SU(3) (dim 8) as a maximal subgroup, and SU(4) contains SU(3)×U(1) as a subgroup. The precise identification of the Standard Model gauge group within the Yⁱ_jk algebra requires Paper 19.

7. Open Problems
The following questions are precisely stated and mathematically tractable:

    • The muon/electron mass ratio. The ratio 206.77 is not yet derivable from Y₃₂₃ alone. Candidate approaches: (a) the cascade amplification factor φ from the ideal-to-monograph collapse; (b) quantum corrections from the full Yⁱ_jk algebra composition law; (c) the ratio of the Hu Gua orbit lengths under the nuclear hexagram operation.
    • Colour confinement. The pure material line {b₁,b₂,η} carries the colour triplet. Confinement requires that observable states have integer charge. The mathematical condition is that only colour-singlet combinations (summing to zero colour charge) correspond to fixed points of Ψ. Proving this from the Y₃₂₃ structure alone is an open problem.
    • The SU(4)–G₂ embedding. Does SU(4) embed in G₂? If not, what is the precise relationship between the gauge symmetry of the material block and the connection geometry of S⁶? The answer will determine whether the Standard Model gauge group appears as a subgroup of G₂ or as a complementary structure.
    • The functoriality of Π. Is the collapse map Π a functor between categories? If so, the lost information can be characterised categorically. If not, the truncation is irreversible in a precise sense, and the simulation cannot be made fully stable within finite-precision arithmetic — only asymptotically approached.
    • Third generation. The Standard Model has three generations of fermions. Y₃₂₃ provides two (eternal modes = first generation, spiral modes = second generation). Where is the third? The nilpotent block provides force carriers, not matter. The answer may lie in the full Yⁱ_jk algebra — specifically in operators at other positions (i,j,k) that share the N|M block structure but differ in generation index.

Y₃₂₃ is not isolated. It is position (3,2,3) in a 64-element algebra — selected from 64 candidates by four axioms, living at the intersection of G₂ connection geometry and SU(4) internal gauge theory, carrying in its eigenspectrum the particle content of the first two generations of matter.

Every floating-point approximation applies Π twice. The mathematics resists not because it is difficult to implement, but because any finite representation loses the phase information that, in the ideal, keeps the spiral modes on the unit circle forever.
The particle correspondence is not fully proved — the mass ratios remain open, confinement is conjectural, the third generation is missing. But what is proved is already extraordinary: the photon is a nilpotent excitation with unit-magnitude coupling; the electron charge is −1 in units of the Observer squared; the colour triplet is the pure material line. These are not analogies. They are mathematical derivations from the four axioms.
The series began with a matrix. It passed through dynamics, algebra, geometry, symbolism, and uniqueness. Now it reaches outward into physics. The matrix was always there. The particles were always in it.
