PART ONE: THE BLOCK DECOMPOSITION

§2. Y is a Direct Sum of Two Invariant Subspaces

The most fundamental structural fact about Y, not previously stated, is that it
decomposes as an exact direct sum:

    Y = Y_nil ⊕ Y_stable

on two complementary, Y-invariant subspaces of ℂ⁷:

    V_nil    = span{τ, a₁, ξ}     (3-dimensional)
    V_stable = span{a₂, b₁, b₂, η}  (4-dimensional)

By Y-invariant we mean: Y maps V_nil into V_nil, and Y maps V_stable into V_stable.
There are NO couplings between the two blocks in the Y matrix. This is verified
by inspection: checking all 12 cross-block entries (the 3×4 and 4×3 off-diagonal
blocks) shows they are identically zero.

The decomposition is exact and algebraic, not approximate. The state space ℂ⁷ splits
as a direct sum ℂ³ ⊕ ℂ⁴ under the action of Y.

§3. The Nilpotent Block Y_nil

The restriction of Y to V_nil = {τ, a₁, ξ} is:

    Y_nil = ⎡  0       −i    −1/√2 + i/√2 ⎤
            ⎢  i        0         0         ⎥
            ⎣ 1/√2+i/√2  0         0        ⎦

All three eigenvalues of Y_nil are zero (verified numerically to < 10⁻¹⁶). The
Jordan structure is determined by the ranks of Y_nil^k:

    rank(Y_nil⁰) = 3
    rank(Y_nil¹) = 2
    rank(Y_nil²) = 1
    rank(Y_nil³) = 0

Decreasing by 1 at each power means the Jordan form is a single block of size 3.
Y_nil is a maximal nilpotent matrix in the 3-dimensional space: the highest possible
nilpotency index for a 3×3 matrix is 3, and Y_nil achieves it.

Y_nil² is explicitly:

    Y_nil² = ⎡ 0   0         0         ⎤
             ⎢ 0   1    −1/√2 − i/√2   ⎥
             ⎣ 0   1/√2−i/√2    −1      ⎦

Y_nil³ = 0 exactly.

PHYSICAL MEANING. The 3-step nilpotency index means that any information initially
held in V_nil is annihilated within exactly 3 Y-applications. The nilpotent block
is a one-way cascade: information flows in from V_stable through the observation
step, passes through the τ → a₁ → ξ → (annihilation) cascade, and is gone. Nothing
accumulates permanently in V_nil.

§4. The Stable Block Y_stable

The restriction of Y to V_stable = {a₂, b₁, b₂, η} is:

    Y_stable = ⎡  0      1      0         0    ⎤
               ⎢ −1      0    1/√2        0    ⎥
               ⎣  0    1/√2    0     −i√3/2    ⎦
               ⎡  0      0    i√3/2       0    ⎦

The eigenvalues of Y_stable are +1, −1, +i√3/2, −i√3/2 (verified exactly). All four
non-nil eigenvalues of Y live here, and here alone.

The internal coupling chain of Y_stable is:

    a₂ ←→ b₁ ←→ b₂ ←→ η
         (antisym) (sym) (antisym)

Three couplings, alternating in symmetry character:
    a₂ ↔ b₁: strength 1 (antisymmetric: Y[2,3] = −Y[3,2] = 1)

The middle coupling (b₁, b₂) is the deviation from so(7). Its symmetry is the

§5. The Role of τ: A Shadow, Not a Substance

The block decomposition reveals something surprising: τ is in the nilpotent block.
It has no component in V_stable. It cannot, therefore, carry permanent information
under Y.

Yet τ is the primary observable quantity — it classifies cells as stone, sand, water,
or air. This apparent contradiction is resolved by understanding the observation step.

At each observation, the state ψ ∈ ℂ⁷ is projected to Re(ψ)/‖Re(ψ)‖ ∈ S⁶. This
projection does not respect the V_nil ⊕ V_stable decomposition: it mixes the two
subspaces. Specifically, the real part of the stable block components (a₂, b₁, b₂, η
components in ℂ, carrying phase from Y-evolution) flows into the normalised real
vector in all seven positions, including position 0 (τ).

In this sense, τ is the SHADOW of the stable block. The stable block evolves under Y,
accumulating complex phase in its components; the observation step collapses this
phase to a real projection; and the normalisation distributes the "weight" of that
projection across all seven positions. The τ position receives a portion of this
weight determined by the relative magnitudes of the stable block's real parts after
Y-application.

The material classification (stone/sand/water) is therefore a classification of the
stable block configuration, made legible through the nilpotent τ as its momentary
representative. Materials are stable block attractors; τ is how they are seen.

§7. The Bifurcation at c = 1/√2

PART THREE: THE OCTONIONS AND g₂

§9. The Octonions in 7 Dimensions

The octonions 𝕆 form the 8-dimensional normed division algebra over ℝ, with
basis {1, e₁, e₂, e₃, e₄, e₅, e₆, e₇} where 1 is the real unit and eᵢ are the
seven imaginary units. Their multiplication is governed by:

    eᵢ · eⱼ = −δᵢⱼ + εᵢⱼₖ eₖ

where εᵢⱼₖ = +1 for the seven cyclic triples from the Fano plane:

    (1,2,4), (2,3,5), (3,4,6), (4,5,7), (5,6,1), (6,7,2), (7,1,3)

The imaginary part Im(𝕆) is the 7-dimensional real vector space spanned by
{e₁,...,e₇}. This is ℝ⁷, and it carries the octonion cross product:

    u × v = Im(u · v)    for u, v ∈ Im(𝕆)

The group G₂ = Aut(𝕆) is the exceptional Lie group of automorphisms of 𝕆. It acts
on Im(𝕆) = ℝ⁷ and is a subgroup of SO(7). It has dimension 14.

§10. The Lie Algebra g₂

The Lie algebra g₂ = Lie(G₂) consists of the infinitesimal automorphisms of 𝕆: the
linear maps D: 𝕆 → 𝕆 that are derivations of the octonion algebra,
D(ab) = D(a)b + aD(b). Restricting to Im(𝕆) = ℝ⁷, g₂ becomes a 14-dimensional
subalgebra of so(7).

The explicit characterisation: g₂ ⊂ so(7) is the kernel of the linear map

    Λ: so(7) → ⊗³(ℝ⁷)*,    (Λ(A))(u,v,w) = −φ(Au,v,w) − φ(u,Av,w) − φ(u,v,Aw)

where φ is the associative 3-form of the octonions (the Fano plane 3-form):

    φ(eₐ, e_b, e_c) = εₐᵦ꜀ ∈ {−1, 0, +1}

The map Λ has rank 7 (it imposes 7 independent constraints on the 21-dimensional
space so(7)), leaving a 14-dimensional kernel. This kernel is g₂.

Verified: the constraint matrix C (shape 343×21, where 343 = 7³ from the 3-form
indices and 21 = dim so(7)) has rank 7, with 14-dimensional right null space — exactly
g₂ ⊂ so(7). The basis for g₂ obtained from the right null space of C verifies:
each basis element annihilates φ to < 10⁻¹⁵ (numerical zero).

PART FOUR: WHY SEVEN DIMENSIONS

§14. The Octonion Dimension Chain

The division algebras over ℝ are:
    ℝ  (dimension 1)
    ℂ  (dimension 2)
    ℍ  (quaternions, dimension 4)
    𝕆  (octonions, dimension 8)

By the Hurwitz theorem, no other normed division algebras exist. The imaginary parts
of these have dimensions 0, 1, 3, 7. The Y₃₂₃ state space is ℂ⁷ with real part ℝ⁷.
The choice of 7 dimensions is therefore the unique dimension that is:

    (i)  The imaginary-part dimension of the largest normed division algebra
    (ii) The dimension preserved by the exceptional Lie group G₂ = Aut(𝕆)
    (iii) Large enough to carry a 4-dimensional stable block and a 3-dimensional
          nilpotent block simultaneously

Any smaller space would lack either the nilpotent cascade (needs dimension ≥ 3) or
the four non-nil eigenvalues (needs dimension ≥ 4). Any larger space would exceed
the exceptional symmetry that makes G₂ possible.

The coordinate breakdown τ, (a₁, a₂), (b₁, b₂), (x, y) — one scalar, two complex
numbers, one complex number — maps naturally onto the octonion structure:
    τ  ↔  one real dimension (the "scalar part" of Im(𝕆))
    φ₁ = a₁ + ia₂  ↔  one complex imaginary direction
    φ₂ = b₁ + ib₂  ↔  one complex imaginary direction
    ξ  = x  + iy   ↔  one complex imaginary direction
    ↓ total: 1 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 7 real dimensions ↓

The mapping 1 + 3 × 2 = 7 divides the 7 dimensions into one "real-like" dimension
(τ) and three "imaginary complex" dimensions (φ₁, φ₂, ξ). This is not an arbitrary
packaging — it corresponds to the decomposition of Im(𝕆) under a specific ℂ ⊂ 𝕆
embedding.

§15. Why the Nilpotent Block Has Dimension 3

The nilpotent block V_nil = {τ, a₁, ξ} has dimension 3. This is the minimum dimension
for a maximal-nilpotency (nilpotency index = dimension) block. Dimension 2 would give
a 2-step cascade; dimension 1 would give immediate annihilation. Dimension 3 gives
a 3-step cascade, which is precisely the period of the nilpotent reset in the
simulation: every 3 steps, the nilpotent component is fully annihilated.

More significantly: the 3-step cascade is the number of steps that separates
observation from the next observation. In the simulation cycle:
    Step 1: Y applied (nil component begins cascade)
    Step 2: Y applied again (nil component advances)
    Step 3: nil component would vanish, but the observation step before step 3
            resets and refreshes it from the stable block

The 3-dimensional nilpotent block creates a 3-step refresh cycle that is exactly
commensurate with the standard observation period. This is not coincidence — it means
that the nil block always carries "fresh" information from the latest stable-block
observation, never stale information from more than 2 steps ago.

§16. Why the Stable Block Has Dimension 4

The stable block V_stable = {a₂, b₁, b₂, η} has dimension 4. A 4-dimensional space
is the minimum that can simultaneously carry:
    — Two unit-modulus eigenvalues (±1): requires ≥ 2 dimensions
    — Two complex-conjugate eigenvalues (±i√3/2): requires ≥ 2 dimensions
    — Total: 4 dimensions minimum

Moreover, the stable block's chain structure a₂ ↔ b₁ ↔ b₂ ↔ η — a path graph on 4
nodes — is the unique chain architecture that allows the critical bifurcation of §7.
A shorter chain (2 or 3 nodes) cannot carry both eternal and spiral modes. A longer
chain (5+ nodes) would create additional eigenvalue pairs not seen in Y₃₂₃.

The 4-dimensional stable block with its linear chain structure is therefore the
minimal structure that generates the required eigenspectrum {±1, ±i√3/2}.

Four coupling strengths appear in Y_stable:

PART FIVE: THE NILPOTENT BLOCK AS OBSERVABLE INTERFACE

§18. The Observation as Symmetry Breaking

The observation step obs(ψ) = Re(ψ)/‖Re(ψ)‖ breaks the ℂ-linearity of the dynamics.
Y acts ℂ-linearly on ℂ⁷; the observation takes the real part, which is only ℝ-linear.
This symmetry breaking is not a defect — it is the feature that makes τ observable.

Without observation: the direct sum V_nil ⊕ V_stable is preserved. The nilpotent
block decays to zero; the stable block evolves periodically/spirally. The dynamics
are completely decoupled, and τ, after 3 steps, is zero forever.

With observation: Re(ψ) mixes V_nil and V_stable coordinates. After projection and
normalisation, the τ position receives contributions from all real parts of all
components. The stable block "leaks" into τ through the observation.

The observation step is therefore a cross-block coupling that exists only at the
interface between the complex Y-dynamics and the real observable. It is the mechanism
by which the eternal stable block makes itself legible through the transient τ.

ψ₀[a₁] = ψ₀[ξ] = 0), after one Y application the state Y·ψ₀ has:

    Y·ψ₀ in V_stable: complex entries from the phase rotations in Y_stable
