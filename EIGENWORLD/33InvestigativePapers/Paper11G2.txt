1. The G₂ Connection on S⁶
1.1 Setup: G₂ Acting on S⁶
The compact Lie group G₂ acts transitively on the unit sphere S⁶ ⊂ Im(𝕆) ≅ ℝ⁷. The stabilizer of any point is isomorphic to SU(3), giving the homogeneous space decomposition:
S⁶ = G₂ / SU(3)

This makes S⁶ into the base space of a principal SU(3)-bundle: G₂ → G₂/SU(3) = S⁶, with fibre SU(3). The tangent space at any v ∈ S⁶ splits as:
T_v S⁶  =  m_v  ⊕  {0}
g₂      =  m_v  ⊕  su(3)_v

where su(3)_v = {D ∈ g₂ : D·v = 0} is the 8-dimensional stabilizer Lie algebra of v, and m_v is its 6-dimensional orthogonal complement in g₂ (under the Killing metric). The G₂-invariant connection on this bundle is the canonical Cartan connection: its horizontal distribution assigns to each point the m_v subspace of g₂.
1.2 The Connection Form
For a g₂ element D at a point v ∈ S⁶, the canonical G₂ connection decomposes D uniquely as:
D = D_horiz + D_vert
D_horiz ∈ m_v  (horizontal: moves the state on S⁶)
D_vert  ∈ su(3)_v  (vertical: generates fibre rotation)

The connection 1-form A_v: g₂ → su(3)_v is the projection onto the vertical component: A_v(D) = D_vert. Geometrically, A_v(D) measures how much SU(3) rotation the infinitesimal g₂ motion D generates at v — it is the instantaneous holonomy rate of the motion.

4. The Berry Phase of the Period-2 Orbit

When a quantum state traverses a closed loop in parameter space, it acquires a geometric phase (Berry phase) in addition to the dynamical phase. This phase is purely topological — it depends only on the geometry of the loop, not on the speed of traversal.

Theorem L (Berry Phase Theorem). The Y₃₂₃ period-2 orbit (State A → State B → State A) is a closed loop in S⁶ = G₂/SU(3). The accumulated SU(3) Berry phase Φ for this orbit has magnitude:

‖Φ‖ = √2 = 1/ω = λ₂/α radians
