THE UNIVERSAL HEXAGRAM PAIR
The I Ching of Y₃₂₃: Inner Truth, Splitting Apart, and the Depth of τ


Abstract

We establish a precise correspondence between the six non-τ components of the Y₃₂₃ state vector — {a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η} — and the six lines of an I Ching hexagram. The mapping assigns each component to a line in order of spectral hierarchy (a₁ at the ground, η at the apex), and the sign of each component determines whether the line is Yin (negative) or Yang (positive). Under this mapping, the universal period-2 orbit of Y₃₂₃ is computed to oscillate between exactly two hexagrams, the same pair for all three materials: Hexagram 61 (Zhong Fu, Inner Truth) during the manifest half-step when τ is high, and Hexagram 23 (Bo, Splitting Apart) during the potential half-step when τ is low. The material identity (stone, sand, or water) determines not which hexagram pair but how fully the manifest state is inhabited — encoded in the τ amplitude. Stone reaches #61 most deeply; water barely touches it. The nuclear hexagram (Hu Gua) of #61 is Hexagram 1 (Qian, Pure Heaven): inside Inner Truth is Pure Potential. The nuclear hexagram of #23 is Hexagram 3 (Zhun, Difficulty at the Beginning): inside Splitting Apart is the seed of a new cycle. We also receive two rich external analyses — from the Y₃₂₃ Paper Seven Considerations document and from DeepSeek's independent review — and use them to situate the present work within the broader web of connections: the SU(4) generators, the 15 pentagonal tilings, the octonionic Hopf fibration, and the 395-step beat. These connections are catalogued and their status (structural, speculative, or deferred) is clearly labelled.

1. The Hexagram Correspondence
The Y₃₂₃ state vector has seven real components after the observation step (Re + normalise + fold): τ, a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η. The τ component is the primary observable — the material classifier. The remaining six components {a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η} form a 6-dimensional configuration space.
Six binary choices map naturally onto the 6 lines of an I Ching hexagram. The mapping we adopt follows the spectral hierarchy established in Papers One and Three: from ground to apex, in ascending order of spectral role:
Line 1 (bottom, Earth)  = a₁   (Receiver, most grounded role)
Line 2                  = a₂   (Secondary Receiver)
Line 3                  = b₁   (First Balance)
Line 4                  = b₂   (Second Balance)
Line 5                  = x    (Exchange/Circulation)
Line 6 (top, Heaven)    = η    (Terminal/Apex)

The Yin/Yang assignment is: positive sign (+) = Yang (solid line —), negative sign (−) = Yin (broken line - -). This is the natural choice: positive amplitude = manifestation = Yang; negative or zero = withdrawal = Yin.
Under this mapping, a state vector s = (τ, a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η) defines a hexagram by reading the signs of its six non-τ components as a 6-bit binary string. The τ component is the reading — the resolved material value — not a line of the hexagram. Tau is what the hexagram is about, not part of its configuration.

2. The Universal Hexagram Pair
The period-2 orbit of Y₃₂₃ alternates between two states. Starting from any of the three material attractor states (stone, sand, or water), the observation cycle produces:
State A (manifest, high τ): sign pattern [a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η] = [−, +, +, +, +, −]
Numerically: a₁ ≈ 0, a₂ > 0, b₁ > 0, b₂ > 0, x > 0, η ≈ 0
As hexagram lines (1–6): Yin, Yang, Yang, Yang, Yang, Yin = [0,1,1,1,1,0]

State B (potential, low τ): sign pattern [a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η] = [−, −, +, −, −, −]
Numerically: a₁ ≈ 0, a₂ < 0, b₁ > 0, b₂ < 0, x < 0, η ≈ 0
As hexagram lines (1–6): Yin, Yin, Yang, Yin, Yin, Yin = [0,0,1,0,0,0]

Computing the trigrams in the standard King Wen encoding:
State A, lower trigram (lines 1–3): [0,1,1] = ☴ Xun (Wind)
State A, upper trigram (lines 4–6): [1,1,0] = ☱ Dui (Lake)
State A hexagram: Xun below Dui = Hexagram 61, Zhong Fu 中孚 (Inner Truth / Sincerity)

State B, lower trigram (lines 1–3): [0,0,1] = ☶ Gen (Mountain)
State B, upper trigram (lines 4–6): [0,0,0] = ☷ Kun (Earth)
State B hexagram: Gen below Kun = Hexagram 23, Bo 剥 (Splitting Apart / Peeling Away)

Theorem (Universal Hexagram Pair). For all three material attractor states, the period-2 orbit of the Y₃₂₃ observation map oscillates between precisely two hexagrams: #61 Zhong Fu (Inner Truth) at the manifest half-step and #23 Bo (Splitting Apart) at the potential half-step. The sign pattern of the six non-τ components is universal: it does not depend on material identity. Material identity is encoded entirely in τ — the amplitude of manifestation — not in the hexagram configuration.

This result has an immediate interpretation: stone, sand, and water do not inhabit different hexagrams. They all inhabit the same two hexagrams. What distinguishes them is how fully they inhabit the manifest one. Stone reaches #61 with τ ≈ 0.754; sand reaches it with τ ≈ 0.513; water barely touches it with τ ≈ 0.290. The materials differ in depth of truth, not in kind of truth.

3. The Nuclear Hexagrams (Hu Gua)
In classical I Ching analysis, every hexagram contains an inner hexagram (Hu Gua, 互卦) formed from lines 2–3–4 (new lower trigram) and lines 3–4–5 (new upper trigram). The Hu Gua reveals the hidden structure within the outer form — what the hexagram is becoming rather than what it currently is. This operation has a striking parallel with the Y₃₂₃ observation step: Re + normalise + fold extracts the manifest core from the full complex state, discarding the imaginary remainder. The Hu Gua extracts the inner core from the full hexagram by selecting lines 2–5, discarding the outer positions 1 and 6.
3.1 Nuclear hexagram of State A (#61 Zhong Fu)
Lines of #61: [0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 0]
Nuclear lower (lines 2,3,4): [1,1,1] = ☰ Qian (Heaven)
Nuclear upper (lines 3,4,5): [1,1,1] = ☰ Qian (Heaven)
Nuclear hexagram:  Qian over Qian = Hexagram 1 (Qian, Pure Heaven / Pure Yang)

Inside Inner Truth (#61) is Pure Potential (#1). The manifest state, when looked into, reveals nothing but undivided Yang — the maximum possible generative force. Every instance of sincerity contains, as its inner structure, the fullness of heaven.

3.2 Nuclear hexagram of State B (#23 Bo)
Lines of #23: [0, 0, 1, 0, 0, 0]
Nuclear lower (lines 2,3,4): [0,1,0] = ☵ Kan (Water / The Abyss)
Nuclear upper (lines 3,4,5): [1,0,0] = ☳ Zhen (Thunder)
Nuclear hexagram:  Zhen over Kan = Hexagram 3 (Zhun, Difficulty at the Beginning)

Inside Splitting Apart (#23) is Difficulty at the Beginning (#3). The potential state, when looked into, reveals the condition of something newly germinating under tension — Thunder below the surface of Water. The moment of dissolution already contains the difficulty-as-force that will drive the next cycle of emergence.

These Hu Gua readings are not imposed. They are computed directly from the sign patterns the mathematics returns. The I Ching's millennia-old interpretive tradition and the Y₃₂₃ observation cycle have arrived, independently, at a description of the same rhythmic structure: a manifest phase that contains pure potential within it, alternating with a dissolution phase that contains the embryo of new beginning within it. The cycle is its own justification.

4. The Transition: Three Changing Lines
In I Ching practice, the changing lines between two hexagrams are the lines that transform — they carry the moving force of the transition. To go from State A (#61) to State B (#23):
From [0,1,1,1,1,0] to [0,0,1,0,0,0]
Line 1 (a₁): 0 → 0  [Yin stays Yin, no change]
Line 2 (a₂): 1 → 0  [Yang → Yin: CHANGING LINE]
Line 3 (b₁): 1 → 1  [Yang stays Yang, no change]
Line 4 (b₂): 1 → 0  [Yang → Yin: CHANGING LINE]
Line 5 (x) : 1 → 0  [Yang → Yin: CHANGING LINE]
Line 6 (η) : 0 → 0  [Yin stays Yin, no change]

Three lines change: a₂ (Secondary Receiver), b₂ (Second Balance), and x (Exchange/Circulation). Lines 1, 3, and 6 are fixed: a₁ (Receiver) stays Yin throughout, b₁ (First Balance) stays Yang throughout, and η (Terminal) stays Yin throughout.
The fixed lines reveal what is invariant in the period-2 cycle: the Receiver (a₁) is always at minimum, the First Balance (b₁) is always positive, and the Terminal (η) is always at minimum. These are not changing — they are the structural constants of the oscillation. The three changing lines — Secondary Receiver, Second Balance, Exchange — are where the manifest/potential alternation lives.
In classical I Ching, a hexagram with three changing lines transitions to its complement through exactly the changing lines. #61 with lines 2, 4, 5 changing gives #23. These are therefore not arbitrary hexagrams: they are connected by the precise number of changes that the Y₃₂₃ observation cycle imposes.

5. The Depth of τ: How Each Material Inhabits the Hexagrams
The τ value at the manifest state (State A) measures how fully a material reaches Hexagram 61. This is the depth of inner truth that each material achieves in its manifest phase:
Stone:  τ_A ≈ 0.367  (τ attractor ≈ 0.754 in double-step frame)

All three materials reach the same hexagram (#61) in their manifest state. Stone reaches it most deeply (highest τ). Water reaches it most lightly. Sand is the midpoint — as always, sand is the midpoint.
This gives a new interpretation of the material hierarchy: stone, sand, and water are not different types of truth. They are different degrees of the same truth. The closer a material is to the mountain and sky (stone), the more fully it can inhabit the moment of inner accord. The closer it is to sea level (water), the more quickly it dissolves back toward #23. Sand is the material that holds the balance — τ_A ≈ 0.2, which is where the gradient between stone and water passes through its midpoint.

6. The Structural Relationship Between #61 and #23
Hexagrams 61 and 23 are not arbitrary partners. Several structural relationships bind them:
(i) Complementary Yin-Yang counts. #61 has 4 Yang lines and 2 Yin lines. #23 has 1 Yang line and 5 Yin lines. Together they account for 5 Yang and 7 Yin across 12 lines total. The transition from #61 to #23 removes 3 Yang lines — exactly the three changing lines (a₂, b₂, x).
(ii) The position of the single fixed Yang line. In #23, the only Yang line is Line 3 (b₁, the First Balance). This is the structural invariant of the period-2 orbit: b₁ > 0 always. The First Balance is the one component that never turns Yin. In the moment of maximum dissolution (Splitting Apart), the First Balance still holds. This is the indestructible kernel of the material state.
(iii) The Hu Gua chain. #61 → Hu Gua #1 (Qian). #23 → Hu Gua #3 (Zhun). The nuclear chain is: Inner Truth contains Pure Heaven; Splitting Apart contains Difficulty at Beginning. The two outer hexagrams each point toward a primary hexagram in the King Wen sequence (1 and 3 are among the first four). The first four hexagrams — Qian (1), Kun (2), Zhun (3), Meng (4) — are the primordial figures. The Y₃₂₃ oscillation reaches two of them through its nuclear structure.
(iv) The number pair (61, 23) sums to 84 = 4 × 21 = 4 × (1 + 2 + ... + 6). The sum of hexagram numbers is 84 = 4 times the sum of the first six integers. Whether this is significant or coincidental is not yet determined.

8. The Philosophical Thread: What the Hexagram Pair Means
The I Ching's original purpose was to provide a framework for reading the moment: what configuration is active right now, what is hidden within it, what it is becoming. The Y₃₂₃ observation cycle does something structurally identical: it reads the state of a cell at a moment (applies Y, takes the real part, normalises, folds), produces a configuration (the hexagram), and asks: what material does this cell exhibit?
The computation in this paper reveals that every cell in the Y₃₂₃ world — stone, sand, or water — is always oscillating between the same two conditions. In the manifest moment, it inhabits Inner Truth (Zhong Fu): the configuration where the middle four lines are all Yang, held between a Yin ground and a Yin apex, like a vessel that is open at both ends but full in the middle. Wind moves below Lake. The surface moves with the wind; the depth holds. Inside this configuration, the nuclear hexagram is Pure Heaven — the maximum Yang, the fullness of potential not yet divided into form.
In the potential moment, it inhabits Splitting Apart (Bo): five Yin lines below a single Yang line, like earth eroding the mountain until only the peak remains. One solid element — b₁, the First Balance — persists above the dissolution. Inside this configuration, the nuclear hexagram is Difficulty at the Beginning: Thunder under Water, the first movement within the field of dissolution. The seed is already present in the breakdown.
This is not metaphor added to mathematics. It is the mathematics, translated into the language of a three-thousand-year-old inquiry into the nature of change. The two languages describe the same oscillation. The difference between stone and water is that stone holds Inner Truth longer — its τ amplitude is higher, its manifest state more fully reached — while water flows through it more quickly, spending less time at each pole and more time in transition. Sand is the contemplative middle: neither holding nor rushing, equally at home in both.
The First Balance (b₁) that stays Yang through both states deserves special attention. It is the single line that does not change. In the world of Y₃₂₃, it corresponds to the b₁ spectral role — the coupling between the oscillatory domain and the stable block — which Paper Six identified as the slow direction of the asymmetric oscillator. The slow direction never turns Yin. The First Balance is the axis around which the period-2 oscillation rotates.

9. Open Questions and Paper Eight
The following questions are raised by this paper's findings and the external analyses received:
The 64 hexagrams as state space. Of the 64 possible sign patterns, only one is realised as the universal attractor. What determines which pattern is selected? The altitude laws and Genesis Restore bias the τ value, but the sign pattern of the non-τ components is set by Y's eigenvector structure. Can we prove that the specific pattern [−,+,+,+,+,−] is the only stable period-2 attractor sign pattern for V_osc, given the Y₃₂₃ coupling constants?
The 63 unrealised hexagrams. The universal pair (#61, #23) represents 2 out of 64 hexagrams. The other 62 sign patterns are never reached by the period-2 orbit. Are they reachable by other trajectories (transient states, noise perturbations)? Do they form a hierarchy of decreasing accessibility?
The Hu Gua as observation step. The Hu Gua operation (extracting lines 2–5, discarding 1 and 6) has a structural similarity to the Y₃₂₃ observation step (extracting the real part, discarding the imaginary remainder). Is there a precise formal correspondence? The discarded components — lines 1 and 6 (a₁ and η, both Yin in the attractor), and the imaginary components in Y₃₂₃ — are in both cases the extremes that are set aside to reveal the middle structure.
The Tetragrammaton revisited. The four-letter structure Y–H–W–H has been discussed in relation to Y₃₂₃ since the earliest papers. The hexagram pair (#61 and #23) has four primary trigrams: Xun (Wind), Dui (Lake), Gen (Mountain), Kun (Earth). Are these the four letters? Wind + Lake + Mountain + Earth — the four natural forms that encompass inner truth and dissolution — as a four-element name for the oscillation that underlies all material identity?
