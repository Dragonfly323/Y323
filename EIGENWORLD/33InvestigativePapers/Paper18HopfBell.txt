2. The Dimensional Chain
2.1 The Octonionic Hopf Fibration
The octonionic Hopf fibration is one of the four classical Hopf fibrations, the largest and most exceptional:
S⁰ → S¹  → S¹    (real, trivial)
S¹ → S³  → S²    (complex)
S³ → S⁷  → S⁴    (quaternionic)
S⁷ → S¹⁵ → S⁸    (octonionic)
The octonionic fibration has total space S¹⁵ — the unit sphere in 𝕆² — base space S⁸, and fibre S⁷. The Hopf map sends (p,q) ∈ 𝕆² with |p|²+|q|²=1 to the point p·q⁻¹ ∈ 𝕆∪{∞} ≅ S⁸. The fibre over each base point is a copy of S⁷ — the unit octonions — acting by right multiplication.
The infinitesimal generator of the Hopf rotation (the right multiplication by e^{iθ} in any complex subalgebra of 𝕆) has eigenvalues ±i on Im(𝕆). These are the ideal spiral eigenvalues of 𝒴_ideal. All four eigenvalues of the ideal material block lie on the unit circle: {+1, −1, +i, −i}. The ideal characteristic polynomial is λ⁴ − 1 = 0. Nothing decays. The Hopf rotation is eternal.
