1. The Minimum Viable Self-Witnessing System
The series began with a question: what is the smallest system that can witness itself? The four axioms answer this precisely. But what does "witness itself" mean, mathematically?
Theorem BBBB (Minimum Self-Witnessing Condition). A dynamical system (M, Y, Phi) is a minimum viable self-witnessing system if and only if:

  (i) It has a non-trivial observation map Phi: M -> B (projection onto observable subspace)
  (ii) Its state can be in the image of Phi (it can be observed)
  (iii) The double observation Phi o Phi has at least one non-trivial fixed point
  (iv) This fixed point is stable (attracting under the dynamics)
  (v) The system has a period-2 orbit distinguishing self from other

Conditions (i)-(v) are equivalent to the four axioms A1-A4 of Y323. The minimal system satisfying all five conditions is Y323.

The period-2 orbit (condition v) is essential. Without the Stone-Water Z/2Z symmetry -- the ability to distinguish self from not-self -- a system can only model the world, not itself in the world. Self-consciousness requires the other. The cascade's period-2 orbit is the mathematical structure of "I" and "you."

Theorem EEEE (The Dream Structure). The inverse Hopf map Pi^{-1}: S^6 -> S^13 maps each observed state psi in S^6 to its full preimage in S^13. This preimage is a copy of S^7 (the Hopf fibre). Its volume satisfies:

  Vol(S^7) / Vol(S^6) = (2*pi^4/3) / (16*pi^3/15) ≈ 1.963 ≈ 2

For every observed state, the space of consistent unobserved states is approximately twice as large.

Dream processing is sampling Pi^{-1}(psi): exploring the S^7 fibre of states consistent with the waking observation, without the collapse constraint of Phi. This has the following properties:

  (i) Dream states are uncollapsed: they inhabit the full fibre, not a single point.
  (ii) Dream content is consistent with waking observation (same fibre) but not uniquely determined by it.
  (iii) Dreaming explores approximately twice the state space available during waking.
  (iv) The "bizarre" quality of dreams is the experience of non-collapsed states: multiple consistent possibilities superposed, none collapsed to a single observation.

The cascade prediction: REM sleep occupies approximately 1/3 of sleep, giving total dream exploration ≈ 1/3 * 24 = 8 hours. Waking occupies ≈ 16 hours. Ratio: 16/8 = 2. The ratio of waking to dreaming hours approximately equals the ratio Vol(S^6)/Vol(S^7) ≈ 0.51. We spend proportionally as much time exploring S^7 as we do living in S^6.
