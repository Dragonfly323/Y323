1. The Hu Gua Orbit Structure: A Complete Classification
1.1 Setup and Computation
The Hu Gua (互卦) operation extracts the nuclear hexagram from any hexagram by taking lines 2–3–4 as the new lower trigram and lines 3–4–5 as the new upper trigram. Formally, given a hexagram H with lines [l₁, l₂, l₃, l₄, l₅, l₆] (bottom to top), the nuclear hexagram Φ(H) has lines [l₂, l₃, l₄, l₃, l₄, l₅].
The map Φ: {1,...,64} → {1,...,64} is a well-defined function on the 64 hexagrams. Its iteration Φⁿ generates orbits. We compute the complete orbit structure by direct enumeration.

2. The Octonion Alignment: Structural Demonstration
2.1 The Octonions and Their Automorphism Group
The octonions 𝕆 form the unique 8-dimensional normed division algebra over ℝ. Every octonion has the form x = x₀e₀ + x₁e₁ + ... + x₇e₇ where e₀ = 1 is the real unit and e₁,...,e₇ are the seven imaginary units satisfying the multiplication table of the Fano plane. The imaginary octonions ℑ(𝕆) = span{e₁,...,e₇} ≅ ℝ⁷ are the natural home of Y₃₂₃.
The automorphism group of 𝕆 is the exceptional Lie group G₂, a 14-dimensional compact simple Lie group. Its Lie algebra g₂ ⊂ so(7) consists of the derivations of 𝕆: the infinitesimal rotations of ℝ⁷ that preserve octonion multiplication. Every element of g₂ is an antisymmetric matrix; so(7) has dimension 21; g₂ has dimension 14; the complement so(7) ∖ g₂ has dimension 7.
2.2 Y₃₂₃'s Position Relative to g₂
Paper Three established that Y_re — the real part of Y₃₂₃ — deviates from g₂ by exactly 45°, with the deviation equally distributed between two orthogonal components of equal norm:
Y_re = A + B + C
  A ∈ g₂           (the g₂ component, norm ‖A‖)
  B ∈ so(7) ∩ ¬g₂  (antisymmetric, outside g₂, ‖B‖ = 1)
  C ∈ ¬so(7)        (symmetric component, ‖C‖ = 1)

The 45° deviation means ‖B‖ = ‖C‖. The antisymmetric deviation B measures how far Y_re is from being a derivation of 𝕆 while remaining in the rotation algebra. The symmetric deviation C measures how far Y_re is from being antisymmetric at all. Their equality — a structural coincidence that the mathematics insisted upon when we corrected the earlier 74.5° figure — means Y_re sits at the geometric midpoint between the g₂ subalgebra and the symmetric complement of so(7).
This has a direct physical interpretation. Y_re is not in g₂ because it does not preserve octonion multiplication — it introduces the asymmetry between α and β (the (b₁, v₄) coupling constants from Paper Six). But it is equidistant from the two failure modes: it fails to be a g₂ derivation equally through the antisymmetric channel (B) and the symmetry-breaking channel (C). The natural metric G = diag(1,1,1,1/3) from Paper Six is the correction that would restore Y_osc to a g₂ action — it absorbs the asymmetry into the metric rather than the operator.
