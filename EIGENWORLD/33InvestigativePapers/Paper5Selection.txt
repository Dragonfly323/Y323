PART I. THE QUESTION
================================================================================

Paper Four proved that the isolated single-cell map has no fixed points with
τ > 0. Material identity is a network phenomenon. The double-step map Φ²
has a continuum of fixed points spanning the full range τ ∈ [0, 1]. From
random initial conditions, a cell converges to arbitrary τ values distributed
across the entire interval.

And yet: at convergence, every active non-air cell in the island system belongs
to one of exactly three materials, with τ values clustered around three specific
values: τ_stone ≈ 0.754, τ_sand ≈ 0.513, τ_water ≈ 0.290.

Why three? Why those values? This paper answers both questions.

The answer turns out to be elegant and involves a principle that deserves its
own name: *Altitude Determines Fate*.

================================================================================
PART II. THE GENESIS ALTITUDE MAP
================================================================================

Every cell in the system carries a genesis position — the spatial coordinate
(x, y, z) where it was born, determined by the island geometry. The z-coordinate
is the altitude above the reference plane.

The Genesis Restore law (Law 6) defines a *home τ* for each cell:

    τ_home(i) = TAU_WATER + (TAU_STONE − TAU_WATER) · z_norm(i)

where z_norm(i) ∈ [0, 1] is the cell's genesis altitude normalised across the
full island height range. This is a linear map from altitude to τ threshold.

    At z_norm = 0 (sea floor):   τ_home = TAU_WATER = 0.15
    At z_norm = 1 (peak):        τ_home = TAU_STONE = 0.65
    At z_norm = 0.5 (midslope):  τ_home = (0.15 + 0.65)/2 = 0.40 = TAU_SAND

This is the first theorem of this paper:

THEOREM (Sand is the Midpoint). TAU_SAND = (TAU_STONE + TAU_WATER)/2. The three
τ thresholds are equally spaced on the real line:

    TAU_WATER = 0.15,   TAU_SAND = 0.40,   TAU_STONE = 0.65
    spacing = 0.25 (equal in both gaps)

Sand is not a separate material arbitrarily placed between stone and water. It is
the arithmetic midpoint of the stone-water interval — the material that corresponds
to midslope altitude, the boundary between highland and lowland dynamics.

The equal spacing means the τ interval [TAU_WATER, TAU_STONE] = [0.15, 0.65] is
partitioned into two equal bands of width 0.25, with sand at the dividing point.
This is a natural trisection of the τ range that supports life (neither stone nor
void).

PART III. THE SELECTION MECHANISM
================================================================================

The Genesis Restore law acts as a *one-sided restoring force*:

    If τ(i) < τ_home(i): restore toward the stone-pole attractor state w_stone,
                          with strength RESTORE_STRENGTH = 0.80.
    If τ(i) ≥ τ_home(i): no force. The cell has found its natural level.

This is a ratchet, not a spring. It only pulls upward, never downward. Combined
with the Sea-Level law, which pushes sub-oceanic cells *downward* when they drift
above the water band, the system has two complementary forces:

    Genesis Restore: τ < τ_home(altitude) → pull UP toward stone pole
    Sea-Level law:   τ > WATER_CENTRE and below sea → push DOWN toward water

These two forces bracket the attractor region. A cell's equilibrium is where
these forces, together with the double-step dynamics and spatial diffusion,
balance.

THE THREE ATTRACTOR BANDS. The island geometry creates three natural altitude
zones:

    Highland (z_norm > 0.5):  τ_home > TAU_SAND → Genesis restore pulls toward stone
    Midslope (z_norm ≈ 0.5):  τ_home ≈ TAU_SAND → Weak restore, sand equilibrium
    Lowland/Sea (z_norm < 0.5): Sea-level law dominates → water equilibrium

A cell born in the highland has a home τ in the stone band (0.40–0.65). The
genesis restore continually pulls it upward whenever it drifts below. The
double-step dynamics can place it anywhere on the fixed-point manifold above
τ_home, but the pull ensures it never falls below. The attractor τ is the
lowest fixed point of the double-step map that lies above τ_home — or, more
precisely, the fixed point selected by the spatial diffusion from the cell's
stone-material neighbours.

A cell in the sea basin has a home τ at TAU_WATER = 0.15. The Sea-Level law
additionally pushes it back toward WATER_CENTRE = 0.275 if it drifts too high.
The equilibrium is in the water band.

A cell on the midslope has home τ ≈ TAU_SAND = 0.40. Neither the highland nor
the sea-level law dominates; the cell is in a transitional zone where diffusion
from neighbours in both directions shapes the equilibrium.

PART IV. WHY THE ATTRACTOR TAUS EXCEED THE THRESHOLDS
================================================================================

The observed attractor τ values are all *higher* than their corresponding thresholds:

    τ_stone ≈ 0.754   vs.   TAU_STONE threshold = 0.65   (overshoot: +0.104)
    τ_sand  ≈ 0.513   vs.   TAU_SAND  threshold = 0.40   (overshoot: +0.113)
    τ_water ≈ 0.290   vs.   TAU_WATER threshold = 0.15   (overshoot: +0.140)

The overshoot is remarkably consistent: approximately +0.10 to +0.14 above the
threshold for all three materials. This is not arbitrary.

The overshoot arises from the DOUBLE-STEP dynamics. The double-step map Φ² is
not identity-preserving at the threshold τ values — it drifts the cell upward
from its home τ through the dynamics of Y's stable block. The attractor state
is where the upward drift of the stable block's observation, the coherence
diffusion from same-material neighbours, and the downward pull of the laws
balance in equilibrium.

More precisely: the double-step at any τ has a *natural upward bias* because
Y's stable block eigenvectors have τ-projections that are enhanced by the
double application. Each double-step tends to increase τ for states in the stone
and sand bands and stabilise it for water states. The laws prevent runaway
increase by providing a ceiling (no upward law for stone, sea-level ceiling for
water). The equilibrium overshoots the threshold by the amount the double-step
naturally raises τ before the ceiling is reached.

The water overshoot (+0.140) is larger than stone (+0.104) despite water having
the weakest material identity (lowest Lyapunov stability from Paper Four). This
reflects the sea-level law's WATER_CENTRE target of 0.275 = (0.15 + 0.40)/2 —
the water attractor is not at TAU_WATER itself but at the midpoint of the water
band, which is 0.125 above TAU_WATER. The actual attractor at 0.290 is near
this midpoint.

PART V. THE THREENESS THEOREM
================================================================================

We can now answer: *why three materials?*

The answer has three parts, each necessary.

PART A: THE ALTITUDE MAP IS LINEAR.
τ_home(z) = TAU_WATER + (TAU_STONE − TAU_WATER) · z is linear in altitude.
A linear map from a 1D input (altitude) produces a 1D family of equilibria.
The question of *how many* stable equilibria reduces to: how many stable bands
does the τ axis support?

PART B: THE DOUBLE-STEP HAS TWO STABLE BASINS.
The double-step fixed-point manifold is a continuum, but the *network-stable*
portions are those where the spatial diffusion reinforces the state — that is,
where neighbouring cells of the same type provide coherent input. This requires
material boundaries to be *stable under diffusion*, which requires a minimum
separation in τ between adjacent materials. The minimum separation is governed
by the coherence threshold and the spatial kernel width.

From Paper Two, the cross-material coherences at convergence are large:
stone-sand ≈ 0.85, sand-water ≈ 0.89. This means adjacent materials are
*not* selected by coherence exclusion — they can diffuse into each other freely.
What separates them is *spatial locality* (the Gaussian kernel, σ = 1.0) and
the altitude-determined restore force.

PART C: THE EQUAL SPACING CREATES EXACTLY THREE BANDS.
The altitude range [0, 1] maps to τ_home ∈ [0.15, 0.65], a range of width 0.50.
The equal spacing of 0.25 between thresholds means the interval is trisected:

    [0.15, 0.40): water band   → sea-level dominates, stable water equilibrium
    [0.40, 0.65): sand band    → transitional, stable sand equilibrium at midslope
    [0.65, ∞):    stone band   → highland, stable stone equilibrium

Three bands, three materials.

If the spacing were not equal — if TAU_SAND were not the midpoint — one band
would be narrower and potentially unstable under spatial diffusion. The equal
spacing maximises the effective separation between materials and makes all three
bands dynamically accessible and robustly stable.

The question then becomes: *why is TAU_SAND the midpoint?*

THEOREM (Midpoint Necessity). Given a linear altitude map with endpoints
TAU_WATER and TAU_STONE, the only value of TAU_SAND that is simultaneously:
    (a) the τ_home of the midslope (z_norm = 0.5), AND
    (b) the threshold between the two restore-force directions,
is TAU_SAND = (TAU_STONE + TAU_WATER)/2.

Proof: Direct substitution. τ_home(z=0.5) = TAU_WATER + (TAU_STONE − TAU_WATER)·0.5
= (TAU_WATER + TAU_STONE)/2 = TAU_SAND. □

Sand is the midpoint *because* it is defined as the equilibrium of the midslope.
The midslope is the altitude at which highland and lowland forces are balanced.
Sand is the material of balance — the τ value where neither the stone-pole pull
nor the water-centre push dominates.

PART VI. THE DEEPER STRUCTURE: V_OSC AND THE ALTITUDE MAP
================================================================================

From Paper Four, every period-2 orbit state lies in V_osc, the 4-dimensional
oscillatory subspace of ℝ⁷. The τ component is the first coordinate of V_osc.
The altitude map τ_home(z) is thus a map from the island's spatial geometry
into the τ axis of V_osc.

The altitude map projects the continuous geography of the island onto a single
real number — τ — which then determines which part of the V_osc manifold the
cell occupies. This is a dimensional reduction:

    Island geography (3D spatial) → altitude z (1D) → τ_home (1D) → material (3 values)

At each stage, information is collapsed:
1. The full 3D position collapses to altitude (the z-coordinate)
2. Altitude maps linearly to a τ target
3. The τ target — combined with the double-step dynamics — selects a material

The V_osc geometry makes this efficient: within V_osc, the τ component
is decoupled from the other three components in the sense that the
double-step can reach any τ value independently of the b₁, x, and v₄ components.
(This follows from the block structure of Y_osc: the (τ, x) block and (b₁, v₄) block
are independent oscillators with the same frequency.) The altitude map targets τ
without needing to specify the full 4D state — the system finds the V_osc state
compatible with the target τ through diffusion and emergence.

THE REMARKABLE CONSEQUENCE: material identity requires only ONE BIT of geographic
information — altitude — to be transmitted from the island geometry to the cell.
The rest of the cell's state (its b₁, x, v₄ components) is determined by its
neighbours and by the dynamics of Y's stable block. Geography shapes τ; dynamics
shape everything else.

PART VII. THE TAU LADDER AS SPECTRAL TRISECTION
================================================================================

The three τ thresholds divide the τ axis as follows:

    0       0.15       0.40       0.65       1.0
    |  void  |  water  |   sand   |  stone  |
              ←  0.25  →←  0.25  →← 0.35+  →

The void region (τ < 0.15) corresponds to the nil state: τ below the sea-level
floor, where the cell cannot maintain coherent material identity. This is the
air state — not a material, but the absence of material.

The stone region has no upper bound set by a law (τ can approach 1). In practice,
the double-step dynamics and the living-Y field prevent τ from exceeding ~0.95.

The ladder has a direct spectral interpretation. From Paper Three, Y's stable
block has eigenvalues {+1, −1, ±i√3/2}. The τ coordinate measures how much of the
observation step's output is concentrated in the τ direction — equivalently, how
strongly the stable block's eternal modes (+1 and −1) project onto τ through
observation.

For the three materials:
    Stone (τ ≈ 0.754): strong projection of eternal modes onto τ
                        → high temporal coherence, long memory (Lyapunov half-life 3.4 steps)
    Sand  (τ ≈ 0.513): moderate projection
                        → medium memory (half-life 1.9 steps)
    Water (τ ≈ 0.290): weak projection of eternal modes, dominated by spiral modes
                        → short memory (half-life 1.2 steps)

The τ ladder is a ladder of *temporal memory*. Stone remembers. Water forgets
quickly. Sand is the threshold of remembering.

PART VIII. THE PERIOD-2 ORBIT AND THE I CHING
================================================================================

The I Ching's fundamental operation is the transformation of hexagrams — six-line
figures, each line broken (yin) or unbroken (yang). The period-2 orbit of the
isolated cell is exactly this: two states alternating, one with high τ (yang,
unbroken, manifest) and one with low τ (yin, broken, potential).

The network resolves this alternation into a definite state — assigns a material
— by determining which of the two orbit states the cell "really" is. This is the
collapse of potential into actuality.

The I Ching's hexagrams are combinatorial configurations of six binary choices.
Six lines, each a period-2 oscillation, in a 7-dimensional space (6 non-τ
components plus τ itself). The configuration space of Y₃₂₃ cell states has
exactly this structure: τ is the "resolved" component (the observation readout),
and the remaining 6 components encode the configuration of the stable block and
the nilpotent block — the yin and yang oscillations that τ mediates.

The I Ching's 64 hexagrams = 2⁶ binary combinations. Y₃₂₃'s non-τ components
have 6 dimensions: {a₁, a₂, b₁, b₂, x, η}. This coincidence is not yet a
theorem. But the structural parallel is exact: a 1D observable (τ, or the
divination reading) resolves from a 6D space of configurations (the non-τ
components, or the six lines). The oracle collapses potential into actuality.
That collapse is what the observation step does, mathematically.

================================================================================

The research notes describe the Tetragrammaton as a four-symbol ordering schema:

    1. The acting principle of observation
    2. What is observed (the collapsed state)
    3. The intended future state
    4. The fulfillment of that state

In the language of this paper:

    1. The observation step (Re-projection, normalisation, fold)
       — the moment of collapse from complex to real
    2. The material identity (τ value, the collapsed reading)
       — which of the three states the cell currently occupies
    3. The altitude-determined τ_home (the geographic prior)
       — what the island "intends" this cell to become based on where it was born
    4. The convergence to the attractor τ (the fulfillment)
       — the actual equilibrium reached through dynamics and diffusion

The Tetragrammaton structure maps exactly onto the four-stage selection process:
observation → material state → geographic prior → equilibrium. The "intended
future state" is not arbitrary or subjective — it is mathematically encoded in
the genesis altitude via the linear τ_home map.

This is the sense in which the island *intends* its cells. Not through agency,
but through geometry: the shape of the island determines what each cell will
become. Altitude determines fate. The intent is encoded in the manifold.

PART X. SUMMARY OF NEW RESULTS
================================================================================

1. SAND IS THE MIDPOINT. TAU_SAND = (TAU_STONE + TAU_WATER)/2 = 0.40 exactly.
   The three τ thresholds are equally spaced with gap 0.25.

2. ALTITUDE DETERMINES FATE. The Genesis Restore law implements a linear map
   from birth altitude to τ_home. A cell's material identity is determined,
   in the limit of strong restoration, by its birth altitude alone.

3. THE SELECTION THEOREM. The system supports exactly three non-void materials
   because:
   (a) The altitude map is linear and creates a continuous τ_home distribution
   (b) The equal spacing of thresholds trisects the viable τ interval [0.15, 0.65]
   (c) Each band has a stable network equilibrium (confirmed by attractor statistics)
   (d) Any fewer than three bands would require unequal spacing, which is destabilised
       by diffusion; any more would require thresholds closer than the diffusion
       kernel width, causing bands to merge.

4. THE OVERSHOOT LAW. The attractor τ values exceed their thresholds by
   approximately 0.10–0.14 for all three materials. This systematic overshoot
   reflects the double-step's natural upward τ drift in the highland and
   transitional regions, balanced by the law ceilings.

5. TAU AS TEMPORAL MEMORY. The τ ladder is a ladder of Lyapunov stability:
   stone (half-life 3.4 steps) > sand (1.9 steps) > water (1.2 steps).
   Higher τ = longer memory of perturbation = stronger material identity.

6. GEOGRAPHY SHAPES τ; DYNAMICS SHAPE EVERYTHING ELSE. Within V_osc, the τ
   component is targeted by altitude while the other three components (b₁, x, v₄)
   are determined by the Y stable block dynamics and spatial diffusion. The
   dimensional reduction: island geography → altitude → τ_home → material is
   a one-bit encoding of geographic identity.

7. THE PERIOD-2 ORBIT AS YIN-YANG. The isolated cell oscillates between high-τ
   (yang, manifest) and low-τ (yin, potential) states. The network resolves this
   oscillation into a definite material identity. The I Ching's hexagrams
   correspond structurally to the 6-dimensional non-τ configuration space.

PART XI. OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================================

1. THE OVERSHOOT FORMULA. Can the overshoot +0.10–0.14 be derived analytically
   from the double-step dynamics and the law parameters? It should follow from
   the Y_osc eigenvalue (±i/√2) and the threshold values.

2. VARIABLE SPACING. What happens if the thresholds are not equally spaced?
   Is there a stability analysis showing that equal spacing maximises the
   robustness of the three-material system? Is it the unique spacing at which
   all three materials have equal "basin width"?

3. DIFFERENT ISLAND GEOMETRIES. The altitude map τ_home(z) depends on the island's
   altimetric profile. A flat island has no altitude gradient → τ_home = TAU_WATER
   everywhere → only water. A step function island has only two altitude levels
   → two materials. The number of stable materials equals the number of distinct
   altitude bands above the sea-level threshold. For generic smooth islands: three
   bands (water, sand, stone) are the natural count.

4. THE I CHING CONNECTION. Can the 64 hexagram transitions be mapped to specific
   trajectories in Y₃₂₃'s 6D non-τ configuration space? The hexagram transition
   rules (changing lines) might correspond to which of the 6 components crosses
   a threshold during a period-2 orbit.

5. THE SINGULARITY ARGUMENT. If the substrate is shared — if all observers emerge
   from the same singularity and interact through the same manifold — then the
   τ_home map is the system's memory of its own origin. The altitude z encodes
   "how far from the singularity this cell was born," and τ_home is the τ value
   corresponding to that distance. The attractor τ > τ_home means the cell
   always exceeds its origin — it becomes more than its birth altitude would
   suggest, through interaction with the network. The overshoot is the measure
   of how much the collective raises each individual above their beginning.
