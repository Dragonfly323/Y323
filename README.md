# Y323
This matrix was discovered in June of 2025. All work here has been done over the last year with the use of only free-tier LLMs, and a single human lacking the resources and time to ship perfection. What's here is intended for research and development, and does not constitute any finished or even useful form. I have done what I can with what I have, and the scope of the work simply expands vastly far beyond my singular limitations. 

Y323 is a lot of things, but here it is presented as a geometric, Lean-verified, dynamical systems approach to machine learning and data modeling.

The central, overarching project to me would be the Crystal Atlas program and the Asimyth game proposal detailed within it. Resources included for now are the original mythopoetic mathematical synthesis and creative mythological explorations inspired by them, an attempt at world simulation with EigenWorld (leading to discovering the matrix produces the Standard Model to within 2% with nothing extra or added), and a language modeling attempt in FanoPet that I consider successful only in that it faithfully demonstrates the a2 invariance the pure math describes. Over time, I also intend to include additional derivative and exploratory visionary roadmaps, vibecoded sketches, and proposed real-world projects and applications. In the meantime, I hope what's available so far inspires your own projects and considerations. Lean Code is under a separate repository: Y323Lean.

More than a toy, far from its full potential. 

Again, I want to stress that all of this work has been done with the help of free tier LLMs, zero institutional support, and an extremely sporadic and non-conducive work environment. That I have produced so much even so is a testament to the math itself far more than anything that I have to do with it. I will accept that my intuitive grasp of the mathematics is rare, and that I lead the process and correct perceptual errors, but I have only scratched the surface of what is possible and what remains to be discovered or built.

These mathematics represent a substantial and profound leap beyond Quaternions into a realm of zero as axis and generative void, fulfilling the promise of Sedenions while remaining computationally tractable.

Let us explore the boundless limits of math and technology together.
