Educational Anarchy: Ascendency


Educational Anarchy is the end state of a society that has transcended the need for arbitrary government due to the ability of each individual within that society to govern themselves.


The difference between Educational Anarchy and every other politic resides in that it recognizes the need for government while simultaneously recognizing how to overcome it. 


This is a difficult goal, but one that will self-perpetuate once achieved. The Educational Anarchist allows for any government prior to the achievement of the end state of Educational Anarchy. Until then, the only duty the Educational Anarchist places upon the government is that the government make the education of the populace its first priority. 


This is also difficult, as any government that seriously attempts to educate its populace is fundamentally abolishing its own usefulness. To educate the governed is effectively an act of suicide by the established government. A government doing this is unlikely. Educational Anarchists, as a rule, do not trust any form of government, but are not necessarily enemies of any government either. 


The more likely reality is that Educational Anarchists, and others who value free thought and education, educate the populace in whatever ways possible until a critical mass is reached, at which point some dramatic change will take place, which in turn accelerates the education of the populace to the point where Educational Anarchy is no longer an idyllic dream. An Educational Anarchy will naturally operate as a direct democracy when it comes to larger decisions. 


Proper education has the following requirements:


1. Sleep.


2. Nutrition.


3. Unlimited access to information.


4. Teaching how to read, and the art of reading.


5. Teaching how to perform basic arithmetic up to algebra. No further unless voluntary.


6. Teaching of geometric forms and their application to reality


7. Teaching of one's native language.


8. Detailed knowledge of one’s own physical structure, its tendencies, traits, weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and strengths.


9. Training in critical thought, logic, and skepticism. Additionally, teaching how to recognize various forms of manipulation, and how to respond. 


10. The fostering of an open perspective, and the knowing of one’s own safety


Someone is “properly educated” if:


* They have good defenses against psychological manipulation


* They are able to think clearly and logically when needed


* They are able to read and comprehend the written word


* They are successfully able to further their understanding of any area or spectrum of knowledge


* And, most importantly, they have an understanding of themselves and how they, as an organism, react to various stimuli and different ways of thinking. 




The Educational Anarchist's Bill of Rights:


1. Guiding Principles


1. No person/individual shall be deprived of freedom of speech or peaceful expression, whether individually or as a group(s).
2. No words, singularly or collectively, shall be placed under any legal restriction or fine.
3. Each person/individual has one voice and may increase that one voice only through harmonious collaboration. 
4. No person/individual may use funds, coercion, violence, technology, or any other method, to deprive, silence, drown-out, deluge, or overshadow the speech or expression of any other person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals.


~


        II. No person/individual, or group(s) of persons/individuals, may deny any non-invasive means, method, or technology—established prior or in the future—from any person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals.


1. Any form of communication once established is forever established, and its base form of operation cannot be limited or restricted in any way. 
2. No form of communication can be priced unduly, namely beyond thrice its base material cost to produce and/or maintain.
3. No form of communication may be tiered by order of efficiency or by amount/quality of service provided; all is offered equally. 


~


        III. No person/individual shall be subject to involuntary labor, except as punishment for a crime.


1. Any person/individual subject to involuntary labor as punishment for each crime shall be required to perform only the labor to which that person/individual was originally sentenced and no more.
2. All labor given as punishment must be explicitly described and detailed at the time of the sentencing.
3. All labor given as punishment must be under the category of public service, and cannot serve to profit any private person/individual or private group(s) of persons/individuals.
a. "Private" is here defined as any person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals making profit, monetary or otherwise, from the labor.
b. "Public service" is here defined as works relating to the health or beauty of the environment, the safety or beauty of infrastructure—conservation, travel, parks, government property, ETC-or other works determined to be in the public's interest. 


        IV. No person/individual shall be unduly persuaded or coerced, by law or otherwise, to provide services or goods to, or accept services or goods from, any other person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals.


1. A person/individual may provide services or goods for any other person/individual if:
a. That person/individual is personally motivated to do so, and does so while respecting choice. 
        Or:
b. That person/individual is in a privately tended space and in coordination with a person/individual who is personally motivated to provide services or goods.




        V. No person/individual shall be persuaded or coerced, by law or otherwise, to craft or agree to a contract of employment with any other person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals.


1. Any employment shall be a contract between the person/individual employing and the person/individual or group(s) of persons/individuals being employed.
2. This contract of employment shall be completely voluntary and brought about through personal motivation of both parties involved, the employer(s) and employee(s), and shall be subject to no statutes, limitations, rules, laws, or any other form or method of limitation upon the powers of the contract, except for Article V, subsections 3, 4, 5, and 6.
3. The contract cannot in any way, shape, method, contrivance, or form be used, whether presently or in the future, to employ any individuals/persons without pay, services, or information rendered in exchange.
4. The contract shall in no way, shape, method, contrivance, or form be used as a means of coercion or persuasion.
5. The contract can detail only one base agreement between the employer(s) and employee(s); a new contract must be agreed upon if either party desires a new base agreement.
6. In order to be legally binding, the contract must be fully understood by both parties, and a personally motivated, verbal pronouncement of understanding must be witnessed by no fewer than two persons/individuals, with at least one person/individual chosen by each respective party.
7. Article V, and all of its subsections, apply to all forms of legally binding contracts.


        X. Any organism—terrestrial, extraterrestrial, subterranean, aerial, aquatic, or cosmic—evidenced to show the ability of understanding or using communication—meaning words or signs—will be granted the status of a person/individual, along with all members of that organism's species and also genus, if the genus is known.


1. All Articles and subsections apply to all persons/individuals, regardless of any identifying factor, whether genus, species, race, ethnicity, religion, perspective, philosophy, political affiliation, sex, gender, or otherwise not mentioned.
2. No person/individual, once so identified, shall be deprived of this identification and be rendered once more a nonperson/non-individual.
