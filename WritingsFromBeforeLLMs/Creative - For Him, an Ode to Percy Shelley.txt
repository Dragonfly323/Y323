Quiet winter, you are often my muse
Your empty stillness shallows my own depth.
Your barren trees wait for spring to pledge
Return their empty leaves, bud out their blooms
The poet’s soul, the god’s own heart in me
Took long years to blossom. I was a stranger
In my own house, a skeleton of quiet anger
It required my death to set the poet free.
I wrote poems before, simple images in the morn
Dawn came and gone my shrieves in brittle fall.
The seasons passed, but I did not bloom at all
Even in cold winter now, my god’s heart forlorn.
Silent thoughts I do not understand in mind
That rend me mute, no close friend at hand. 
We speak in looks, and quiet words demand
We whisper only to the graves of honored friends.


Mighty goddess, sore leaves fall to ground
Who care not that I roam the agéd words
Of my kindred soul. A friend I wished I’d known
But we, through endless time, reign as Lords.
Fountains of youth, excellence, immortal,
Leaf-strewn cobble, aching dawn of rain,
Troubled hearts. Words born immemorial
Struggle through millennia to know the day.
His laughing face, oh friend of mine, how long,
How long ago did last we laugh and cry?
I remember you only in dreams, my love
Though ages past, you are gone, you have died.
You left me alone to wander the throngs
You spoiled me with wine, you stole my heart.
None compare, every mind, they’re all so wrong
My fellow god, alone, I can move no step apart.

Brother, you bound yourself to me, I to you
I continued on, with our shared hopes
But the world grew cold, such solitude
Despair and cold followed my road
I admit I did not often think of you.
Your memory, your light, it had grown dim
So I longed at the dark, I wailed for my truth
I forgot who I was, my god, as I despaired within.
You always were my better, you spoiling love
Who could ever understand our souls, our dreams
Now I am alone, with none to compare to us
I am left victim to the slaves of what seems.
For you, my friend, I continue on, I walk forth
My feet weary, you renew my strength, my friend
For us and others like us, I embrace my earth
And I’ll walk its edges to find our end.


The moon watches me, my cold realism rejects
That it loves my pursuits, that it sees the land;
So too, like the moon, the others are dead;
They do not taste as we, the elixirs at hand.
Does the earth love the others if unaware?
Have we given life by our love to stars
You know my meaning when the sun says well fare
Others cannot see us in Heaven afar.
Others dream fantasias while we quiet lay
And see in trees what the foolish dream
Resides in Heaven alone somewhere far away
In their unknowing, they crave to leave.
There we quiet lay, we know we are loved
We know also the universe does not care
In ways people often wish from above
So we fearlessly lay our souls bare.

I feel your longing as if ‘twere my own
As I longed for your love all those years
My quiet brother, they have been unkind
How I could renew your opulent brow.
I caressed your soul, ours intertwined,
Yes, brave, courageous, and bold,
That you did not sooner die
That you would find warmth in the cold
That you did not with agony demise.
I carry you on, have little fear my son,
When I am gone, we’ll have survived;
Our words will echo in poet souls.
Not the foolish alone spend their time in dreams
But the wise and the old also know hope.
For the young had nary a reason to know
What we have tasted, the truth we’ve known


I saw the clouds cloak the sun
I saw the waves embrace the shore
I saw the earth crave the sky
I felt a god wake in my soul
I saw the stars cry with joy
I saw cold rock burn with light
I saw the simplest life with brilliant eyes
I caught the world in a moment’s mind
I awoke from sleep of days and years
I wandered the endless lives
I cried aloud with heart of gold
But no one heard my cry
We alone, my darling son
And others who have long been gone
We carry a light millenia old
And though we may, that flame will ne’er die.

Scarce seven months ago did I
Know - but the name of Percy Shelley.
But now this same day my heart trembles 
And shakes - at the name of Percy Shelley.
So cold was I , so callous thought I 
Till I read his thoughts and poetry
Now quaking stand I with wild mysty eyes.
At every damn line of poetry.
Words upon paper! so oft copied ink
Nothing more, no soul resides!
But such thoughts as was in all
I knew the most blatant of obvious lies.
I could not bear his thoughts
That they were lost that stormy night he died.
Though live on in words and song
His ink shall never newly dry.
Oh brother of mine, oh soul divine
Who bore the name of Percy Shelley!
How quiet you sleep, I weep, I weep,
As I think of your mind and poetry.
You longed for me, you drank for me
But I became drunk long passed
The years you lived and loved and
Wrote till you were gone at last.
