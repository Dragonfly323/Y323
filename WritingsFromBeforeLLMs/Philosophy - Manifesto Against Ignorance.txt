Manifesto Against Ignorance
The Importance of Possibility and the Reality of Psychological Warfare in the Information Sea


Introduction


        Life is built on perception. The world we perceive is often not the world that actually exists. Every day, we are subjected to influences that threaten to alter our perceptions and distort our view of reality. Every opinion, view, and piece of information can affect some change in how we perceive the world around us. The factors that influence us are emotional, mental, and psychological. We are moved to tears by images of war and violence without knowing if they are real or fake. We take what is given us at face value. This is killing us. 
        The lack of questioning and doubt of these sources of information so blatantly forced upon us is fatal to the freedom and prosperity of humankind. Newsstands are filled with headlines and front page pictures that paint a narrative, and we too often accept that narrative as truth. There is rarely, if ever, a truly alternative view to the mainstream focus that we will see in our day-to-day life. The reality of our subjection to information that we have neither sought nor for which we have asked presents the most dangerous foe that liberty has ever faced.
        If alternative sources of information are not actively sought, an individual will have no choice against the views manufactured by advertisements, society, and media. In any society, an alternative to that society will never naturally arise from within the society itself. Homogeneity, or uniformity, is death and stasis. Were it not for exposure to new ideas from outside a particular society, that society would rarely, if ever, elsewise experience change. Slavery, for example, will remain forever if a society of slavery is not exposed to the idea of freedom. 
        That is exactly what we, as a race, as a species, face today. There are those who work against our interest for their own advancement, who have no desire for our well-being or benefit or freedom or peace or prosperity. We are exposed without consent to opinions and images that tell us to think and believe and adopt certain perspectives. We are subjected to advertisements that tell us what to buy and why we are wrong or lacking to not. We are told that we must change ourselves to fit the narrative. This is a crime against humanity.
        The people who do this do not care that we fall into debt and unhappiness. They do not care that we are victims of violence and suppression and oppression. They do not care that we fight and bicker amongst ourselves. They actually support such division. Our basic instinct of tribalism is daily used against us, whether through sports or politics or groups or agendas. At heart we all desire the same. We desire peace first and foremost and are cajoled into war only when we are made to feel threatened. We are told we cannot work together. 
        We cannot work together, not as we are now. We cannot work together because we have not been exposed to that idea of unity. But the bastardization of the idea has been exposed to us to our detriment and downfall. We are told by our true enemy that we must work together to defeat some other group, or agenda, or outlook upon life. All the while, we are thrust at odds against each other over superficial grievances and matters that do not affect positive social change. While we fight amongst ourselves over matters of race, religion, and politics, we are daily exploited by the disease of consumerism, by the bankers, by the ones who poison our food for profit. 
        Our cause is a just one, but we never follow it. While the forests are burning around us, we bicker over which color our tents should be. The enemy, the true enemy, has turned us against ourselves. They have done this through their control of information. They flood the information sea with half-truths lies, misinformation, and distractions. They report on the news the terrible going-ons from across the world in an effort to make us cower and relinquish our sovereignty to a foreign power–our own government. And we cower to them.
        How disheartening to see how simply the people go about their lives! It should be a thing of joy. It is not. Daily are their liberties stripped from them, every day as they relinquish their power for protection. Why? Because they have no alternative. They are told through the news, the papers, through social media, and through reinforcers of a diseased seed that a certain perspective is true. The people make no effort to seek out alternatives or to question what is being told them. Sometimes, they even believe they are fighting the evil tyrants, but they are actually falling into their game. The people join groups that are trees grown from seeds planted by the true enemy.
        There is no break from conditioning but by curiosity. So many things try to crush that curiosity. We are given our school, our bread, our circus, our drugs, and our television. We consume incessantly and are never filled. We are cursed and derided for questioning what everyone else accepts without question. Some are even jailed or executed for their search or effort to bring an alternative perspective into light. What knowledge is so dangerous that it cannot be allowed?         Lies cannot hurt any truth you hold. Let us allow knowledge to test itself.
        Those who have tasted truth in any subject know that lies are like grains of sand against the ocean; they can only raise it higher. Liers alone suppress alternatives, because they hate the truth, or love the lie. Those who hold the truth welcome any attack. Truth is sure. Truth is firm. Truth is immutable. No darkness can cover the light of a single candle. The suppression of speech, the flooding of false information to obscure true information, and the bombardment of influences all serve to enslave each and every one of us. Truth is liberty.


The Importance of Possibility


        We, the gardeners of truth, those who value what is right and just, till daily our crop. Those who wish to enslave humankind and subject all to violence, strife, and suffering for profit seek to find and destroy our crop, but they cannot. They succeed, however, in that our yield, though pure, is little, while their yield is much and attractive, but secretly empty and poisonous. We bring our crops to market, but all the stalls are taken. We find a corner to give away our wares for free, knowing with a patient resignation that only those who seek us will consume the truth. All the market-goers, by habit, walk in the thoroughfare, and buy the poison they think is life. Such is the easiest path for them.
        There are many others in the back corners, those who grow weeds and sell rusty cans and fool’s gold. Those who seek us will pass by them. Having eaten the crops of the thoroughfare all their life, they will not know what is good and what is rotten. All is strange to them. The thoroughfare daily warns them of the dangers of those who sell their wares in the shadows and the corners, and many do trade the poison of the thoroughfare for the poison of the shadows. This is the origin and strength of cults and violent radicals who do not understand the nature of humankind. It takes much work to distinguish the wheat from the chaff, the gems from the clay.
        We are driven from the thoroughfare whenever we try to give out our wares of truth and liberty and peace, for our fruits are strange to them. Many others come, who do not understand truth and liberty and peace, and are likewise driven from the thoroughfare. Rejection is not judge for truth. The thoroughfare-goers cannot tell how we are different. This is understandable. This is why curiosity is needed. Curiosity drives the search for truth, and the truly curious will never be satisfied by belief or ideology, which is not what we offer. The truth is not belief or ideology.
        But still, we must bring our wares to the thoroughfare, we must give others a glimpse of another life, another way, however few prick their ears to our voices. When they eat bread all their life, and are told that all other foods are wrong, they must see that others eat different foods, and drink different wines. There are many who do as we do, but their foods are just poisons of a different name. Many fall for these other poisons, and few come to eat of the truth.
        Our wares are like the faerie food; once eaten, nothing else will ever satisfy. This is so because only the fruit of truth is actually satisfying. Those who eat of it only come into an awareness of the emptiness all else has always had. Our wares are lonely wares. They are so good and pure that, once partaken, even the thought of the empty soma of the thoroughfare is sickening. Those who seek and find truth lose many friends for this reason; there are few fellow partakers to lift the resulting isolation. A small price to pay.
        Despite the hardship of our labour, and the small numerical return. It is the only labour worth doing. How many lives could be saved if those who drink sugar could be shown how delicious water alone can be? There are many truths that manifest from simplicity. The myth of the thoroughfare is that we need what it offers. Our labour is to offer the alternative, with a quiet voice among the crowded throng, that the thoroughfare lies. Few hear us. Fewer understand our message. Even fewer accept it. But they could never know if we did not offer it to them. This is why it is important that we fight against the overwhelming flood of those who seek to silence and obscure the truth. 


Psychological Warfare in the Information Sea
        
        We are the greatest enemy of those who wish to emaciate the spirit of humankind. We are the sole, true rebellion against the darkness of falsity. We do not name our truth. Our truth will name itself. When people see what is a lie, and what is a distraction, and what is a half-truth, they will come to find the truth that cannot be dismissed or defeated. This truth resounds in all, and in every moment those evil tyrants seek, snap snap, to distract us from it. In all that they do, they seek to lead us down the path of emptiness. They know that the truth overcomes their ability to control.
        In order to prevent the truth becoming known, those evil tyrants who seek only to enslave us use every means they have. We are placed in school against our will, whether by law or social pressure, and taught the virtues of authority and not to question. We are fed with the meat of sickly animals and eat our weight in sugar every year. Our bodies become sickly, and so do our minds. We spend our childhood in institution, and our remainder doing work, or conditioning ourselves through media. Our brains are so exhausted from drudgery and poison that we have no energy for anything else.
        Our sight is violated every where we go, by power lines and roads, by billboards and advertisements, and we are taught from an early age to want what we do not need. We are taught that authority takes care of us. The media we willingly watch assaults us with images meant to appeal to our base instincts, meant to manipulate us for profit. They tell us we will be happy with this or that thing, even though they do not desire our happiness at all. If happiness increases profit, they will pursue it, but the truth is that dissatisfaction drives the greater sale.
        No vast conspiracy is required to know that we are ruined through profit. Are we not all guilty? But from whence came originally this idea that we need beyond our basic needs to be happy? Those in business often desire profit despite of others’ happiness and fall victim to the same crimes they force upon others. Beyond our basic needs, what desire have we? We cause others to desire and succumb to that desire, so that we may have the ability to succumb to our own desires. The great deception of this all is the craving for more.
        Those who seek to enslave humankind use fear and division to control us beyond our squalid consumerism. We are told to buy, and so we buy. We buy beyond our needs to fill the emptiness within. It fills us a little but evaporates soon after. Thereafter, they frighten us with seizure of what little we think we have left. We are told daily of wars and murders and financial instability. We are told of soviets, or now terrorism, some problem so great that no individual can face it alone. So we allow authority more power. They make us afraid and we let them build a cage around us, thinking it protects us from the world. 
        These two emotions master us, the desire for more than what we have and the fear that what we have will be taken away. Beyond this, they use volume. They attack us from every angle of our daily lives. Commercials and advertisements struggle constantly, with great success, to inject themselves into our lives. How can we resist the abuse of curiosity to learn something useless when it is thrust into our face? We live and we have no choice what appears in the sky upon a billboard or upon a tv in a public place. Many, bizarrely, choose this state of things consciously. They have habituated to this invasion.
        When we wish to think ourselves informed, we listen to the news, but the news is all lies, misinformation, distraction, or half-truths. Those aspects of our society that truly harm us are never discussed. We are harmed for profit. Those who make the profit do not allow questioning or discussion. Whether in newspapers, television, or the internet, we are shown one story immediately. This is the thoroughfare of our subservience.
        The internet has saved us so far. Since the internet is not controlled, and anyone can have a voice in it, alternatives can find a place at the market. Those who seek to enslave us combat this availability of information with all their means. They discredit the alternatives, deny their veracity, and misportray their validity. But the greatest weapon they use is silence. 
They never mention the alternatives, because they do not want to spark curiosity. They will never inject a new idea into the sphere. They will only combat it after it has gained independent recognition.
They flood the internet with poison of no substance, lies and distractions. Repetition has always been a great tool of control. We see from various sources, which are really all the same source, the same story, the same narrative, and it is the narrative offered to us easily. It is the narrative we accept because we are aware of no alternative. Those who look find the alternatives, but most, who are conditioned into subservience and trusting belief, never bother to look and think it not worth their time.
Those who wish to enslave us use all these manners of psychological warfare. They use lies, misinformation, fear mongering, half-truths, association of topics with negative and unrelated topics, the use of image and video manipulation to show fabrications and exaggerations, silence on matters of importance, motivating group animosity toward other groups, misreporting sources and locations and causes, the use of unverified sources, various means of establishing credibility without legitimacy, and various means of emotionally manipulating the populace through fear, sympathy, pity, and anger. All of these methods are meant to destroy and undermine critical thinking, curiosity, and alternative perspectives. Most commonly, if you are not for us, you are against us, they say under their breaths with venomed tongues.
If the people recognize these various means of psychological warfare and manipulation, many of which bypass the conscious mind and appeal to instinct, they shall have taken a great step toward freedom. If they do not, they shall be forever slaves to instinctual reactions and fallacies of thought engineered by those who understand the human mind. 
                                    Conclusion


        We all must make great effort to learn who we are. If we know who we are, and how our minds respond to influences, we may be better equipped to disregard attempts of manipulation. When the people allow themselves to be subjected without question to these myriad influences, the perpetrators of which desire only to profit from us, they relinquish their freedoms and securities unknowingly. Death will be our end as a free people if this gradual wearing down of our liberties continues. Already, too many are enslaved.
        The people may think themselves happy in what they do, and to a degree they may be. With each generation, however, our liberties are less. As our liberties grow less, we know less of what alternatives exist. If slaves do not know of freedom, how can they be discontent in their situation? If we are in constant pain, how can we complain if we know nothing of the absence of pain?
        The truth is meant to breed discontent. Even the smallest amount of truth begins this. Without truth, we submit to our masters and join the deafening silence of the main thoroughfare. One day there will be no one in the corners and shadows offering the good-laboured fruit of truth. Our children’s children may have some semblance of happiness in the dark absence of an alternative. Like those who live in frigid reaches all their lives, they will never know that a breeze can bring warmth like a fire. Like those who live beneath city lights, they will never know the sea of stars that no image can fairly capture. They will be content in the absence of an alternative, however unhappy they are. This is the death of all that is good. It is the final defeat of the human spirit.
        Those who taste the truth will always fight for the truth. The truth is good, noble, loving, kind, and peaceful, knowing no belief or ideology and so belonging to no group. When we fight for the truth, we do not fight a war of violence, we fight a war of salvation. We are faced with a dying world, a world that every day strips another layer of freedom from the unsuspecting people. Our common enemy divides us, and uses every mean at hand to halt our ever-advancing march. Our road is long, and our truth cannot be spoken, but we can show the falsehood, and we can wear down the mountain of ignorance and enslavement. 
Ours is a noble and peaceful cause. We march day by day against a massive information sea. For now, and it is the simplest of things, we mean only to cause people to question, to doubt, and to be curious. Truth will stand sure against all tests, and the truth will be our emancipation.
