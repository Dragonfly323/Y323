The laws of nature are so good and the way of life so divine that if anyone willfully keeps another ignorant of these ways, they are guilty of the most heinous crime upon all the earth. The knowledge of spheres and the path of the gods whose invariable ways move beneath our feet may reckon be to some a dangerous truth. However, the willful occlusion by those in power to prevent and dissuade others from learning the oscillations of atoms and stars and their significance to the human element is no deterrent to violence and evil but rather a precursor to it. No man or woman can cause harm upon that which they know. All harm comes from misunderstanding. We have more children than we can feed because we do not see the fields and their limits. We hurt others and war because we cannot see how we war against ourselves and so do ourselves harm. We cannot see the others in ourselves. 


The path of the righteous and the path of the pure is that which all men walk. Every life walks the path, both the rock upon earth and the bird within heaven. There is no harm save that which we add ourselves, the obstacles we place in our own paths because we are told it is not easy, or that we must walk in a certain manner to advance the turn of time. Sit still, I ask of you, and watch how time goes on. Be silent and watch how the birds sing in that absence you have left, and how when the birds are quiet, the insects, and when the insects fall silent, and all other things, you will hear your own blood flowing. Know that silence and music are kindred. Strife and peace are brothers. Neither are true. They are one thing. See how everything flows in a ceaseless changing as a singular, unstoppable force. See how the atoms build towers and sand and oceans and trees and clouds and skies and stars and life. Is it no marvel that such things come from three? Complexity blinds the complex. When you see things simply, you will see them true.


It is no conscious will that adds to life, that blinds and distracts. No consciousness could impose a lack of learning upon others. The birth never ends and that which begins will birth endlessly. When leaders are blind, they have not the way of life and do not see the others in themselves. They are alone. All the ills that fall upon this world are blind. They steal from themselves with every gift they deny others. They do not know what they are doing. They cannot. 


The way of life is infectious. It is the greatest disease, and has the greatest will to spread. No evil can come from it and it cannot deny itself. It even lives in the vile things, but they do not know it. It lies beneath everything. Only excess prevents its light. Excess leads to excess. All things fall away. Time shows no constraints in its expression. The dog knows it as well as the hound, the pig as well as the swine. Names have no meaning to it. It calls everything by its own name. 


The child from birth sees it daily, and every day till the day of death. It is what we have never not seen. That is why it is hidden. All else we know by lack, but in this lacks nothing. Therefore we must see it differently. We must look not by its lack but by its presence, seeing all things as they are. It is limitless, so we can never find where it is not. We can never find it in words, though it is the cause and effect of every word that speaks it. Every word speaks it. This is the nature of a limitless thing. When you take the pudding from a bowl, the bowl appears plainly, and yet its purpose is in holding the pudding. All that you strip to find it, you find to be a part of it. It must nevertheless be stripped. All that covers it is a part of it, but a picture seen closely is not a picture at all. All that is seen is true, but only when all is seen is it understood.


        By its very laws is it limitless, and so as well is it confined. Here is the same as there. Time and place do not occlude it. All things are ever present. This is the nature of a limitless thing. The smallest part of it shows the truth of the whole. If you see the whole in the smallest part, you will understand. It does not flee the dying. It gives birth to the unborn. All that exists exists plainly and you need no knowledge to understand. It is knowledge that hides it. It is the origin of all knowledge; yet, having always been with the origin, we do not recognize it as such. If we do not relinquish our knowledge, we cannot know its origin. We fall upon knowledge, and it shields us from the base. If we take away the buffers that knowledge provides, we shall fall straight to heaven. 


This is the nature of a limitless thing. It will find us when we become like it. We are always like it, but we do not find it. Is this such a mystery? Remember again that it is what we are also like that occludes it. We are too much the pieces and not the whole. We make for ourselves a painting of the world with colors stolen from it. We build walls with the very stones that it provides. The painting becomes a piece of the world, and the wall a part of it. So we know that all things are true. 


All things are true but the one thing is never understood. Every leaf will tell you the same. The winds whisper it in your ear. All things come from one thing. It is an ancient truth. All things remain the same. It never changes. It is endless change. It is no mystery to those who understand. To them, it is perfectly clear. But how did they come to understand? They desired to. It is most often missed because it is detested. Most do not wish to see it. They do not wish to see it because they do not understand it. The child cries against the medicine. The injured dog snaps in retaliation to those who wish to help it. These are clear to those who see them.


It is no crime to live without the way of life. It is painful and unfortunate, it tears the walls of the soul, but it is no crime. The king lives thus. The beggar lives so. The way of life enwraps them all, but they do not know it. Every life is its child. But so few children return.
