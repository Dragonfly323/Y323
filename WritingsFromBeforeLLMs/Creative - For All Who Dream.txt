We are animals, we think and breath
Living in fear of eternity
For we have no place in that endless change
Once come, then gone, and not again.
But what is love, that it should so cause
Heartbreaking ache and unceasing woes
When love itself is utmost care
And we lack it seeming everywhere?


Could love itself transcend all laws? 
Is passion pure, unbridled, raw
For our good? Or will we fall
If overflowing heart gives love to all?
When light so pure and soft and warm
Causes those we love the slightest harm
What trouble we face to hold aloft
That sacred truth we know we ought. 


~~~


What a time, when the winds through the trees
In these late winter days do whisper to me
Of some kinder place where thoughts may be thought
Where I’m not distracted, and can do what I ought.
Confined by the contracts of my ancient past
I walk a thin road, a wearisome path.
Whither it leads, I’m told greater things
But find reason fails to give honest ring
From the bells of purpose, and lovely fate
Shoulders upon me the sweetest dreams
Of beauty incarnate, and so I am late.
My forgotten assignments slip into the stream.


The soft and gentle breeze as I lay still asleep
Gives me greatest joy and all that I need
A little hunger I have, and a little chill
But the joy of life keeps me warmer still
Than all those bundled and slaving long
Into their twilight years who sing no song
Yet believe they do what’s right, what’s good
Without considering the difference it would
Make to the world if we loved art and love,
More than working till we’re so far above
The oceans that we cannot feel their breeze
And we starve for lack of love’s true liberty.
Life so noble so pure and kind, that treats all
Equally, so that we rise and that we fall
The rich and the poor, the slave and the king
How can any find it uninspiring?


We wandered the wastes for countless years
And found past that time that life was near
Upon every turn, and every rest, and the sky
Teemed with countless stars, and gleaming eyes
Of some vast intelligence so far past our own
But in all that greatness we found a home.
We were not strangers, but lovers still
Of our father, our mother, the universal thrills
Of life that would not abandon our wayward souls
Though millennia past, and we had grown old
Into some doddering peasant blind to the riches
Of eternal life that ever besieges
Our fortress of ignorance and pain and will
To accept that lie of ex nihil
When all around us ever changing
The Eternal Will, the endless raging
Seas of fury and cooing babies,
Placid sailors in warring navies,
Endless nights upon distant moons,
And fiery storms of silver spoons.
Pink petals from Cherry trees falling
In death becoming so enthralling
And we’ve no place left that we can see
That does not know eternity.
A ceaseless change ever turning
The silent night into trembling morning
The dirt and soil and air to trees
And the fruit and meat and drink to we.


What terrible passion within me swells
To sing to the morning and the morning star.
When all around me groan of Hell
I know myself that they’ve not far
To go to know that all is well
And Heaven find right where they are.
But we alone, we fortunate we,
Sons and daughters of liberty.
We cannot lay fast our hold
To rip the chains from off their souls
And so helpless joy alone in dust
From stars and life that did form us
And ache and ever we all do long
That others hear the melodic song


To know the embrace of eternity
And sing sweetly the joys of liberty.
To live no longer in quiet infamy
And hate for self and life and God
Fearing the cruel authoritative rod
That beats upon mind and heart and bod.
To fear death and solitude and the truth
Choosing illusions against the solid proof
That all that exists will not cease to.


How few how far how much land and sea between
The children of celestial majesty?
When joy and peace reside in all we see
Yet surrounded by the blind we’ve not one to speak
With of that enrapturing truth that frees
The deadened mind and low brought heart.
That truth where we know what quite we are,
The quaking being of the sea and earth,
When all about us firm is girth
The exulting universal part
Of all that I be and all that thou art.




~~~




I’ll paint a frame so perhaps you can see,
By means of occlusion, what I cannot speak.
That beauty divine that sweetly exists
Cannot be confined, not even a bit.
Like holding the sun so that you might
Share its sweet warmth, but the night
Lingers over those who cannot divine
To exit their caves and kiss the sublime.
It’s always been there, as earth silent sits
But no poor soul is noticing it.
All of life you’ve ever known,
Sadly by perspective clothed
In sickly filters you’re unknowingly thrusting
Upon its pure joy. Then you view it unjustly
As some ill gotten gift you can only despise
And so go through your days believing a lie.
If only you could see all as it is,
And embrace sweet love in enrapturing bliss!




~~~




Oh bitter wound, that bleeds so pure
With heart abreast ‘pun flaming ground
Trembling passion and thoughts of her
Make quake my soul and heaven found.
The trees give quiet, little shakes
When first the brewing storm alights
Upon the clouds that lightning make
And thunder follow as it dies
The darkness of the whispering head
That gives to earth the heav’nly rain,
And cause black hearts to ponder deep
Souls of sadness and raging pains.
Oh breathless embrace that we could make!
Destroy all that we know, bitterness held dear,
Until in the ash, and in the disgrace
We find the warm jewel forged of our tears




~~~




If you knew, as I do,
That blesséd land upon the moon
You’d love the trees, the sun, too
If you knew, as I do.
If you drank of that ancient well
Of which all the true saints have tried to tell
You’d see you’re in heaven, that you make your own hell
If you drank of that ancient well.
You’d see the world in a different eye
Passed the rules and blatant lies
The beauty of the smallest flies
And know that you can never die.
Immortality rests in your hands
And god awakens within your lands
You’ll realize then that you’ve been dead
Awaken and arise, young fighter, from out your bed.
Total freedom cloaks your being
No cruel pain can force your slaving
You’ll find everything worth having
Resides in the purest nothing.
If you knew, as do I
The Divine nature of banal light
You’d see the world without the lies
If you knew, as do I.




~~~




What could in Heaven or within from Earth
Find perfect words that embrace all my joy
Capture true and sure my marvelous mirth
More than any love for Helen of Troy
All those ancient loves from poetic pasts
Held the greatest heights of the living soul
But if our love ever faded at last
Would my fragile heart from that mountain fall?
If the trees could speak, they might speak of time
Whispering in winds and seeking the sun
So I through the years shall find that I pine
To know the depths of the passionate one.
All veins of my heart pour into the sky
But all that escapes is a pitiful sigh.


~~~


I laugh and you laugh in cemented halls
When all we know is what we seem to feel
But not beyond, we hesitate to call
To trust, to share, to forsake Liberty,
Hesitant divines who ne’er know defeat
At constant odds their spirits soar and fly
On silken wings and quick embering feet
To differ in goals. So battle in fie.
Wild with passion, eyes gleaming in the sun.
No one daring move for fear they become
Unwrapped at seams and crumbling fall down.
So fear their souls but too enwrapped their love
And the god who, desiring their cup,
With the other fought, who would not be drunk.




~~~




A salty breeze paints our lips,
Sand dances on our thighs,
The sun peers through the breeze,
And they stole time by sips.
A thousand miles away,
A tree grows alone.
They wondered why I’d say
I’ve missed out on home. 
The waves beckoned me then
To wade into their frothy grins
To bid the sandy world farewell
And then never be seen again.


A vast and ancient world I’d find
In the salty sea and peaceful brine,
The coral reefs so deep below
Where the sun must once have shone.
The world below is quiet and cold;
Monsters roam about unseen.
If I had the eyes to peer within
That ancient darkness- wait, again?
It beckoned out of my quiet mind.
I wanted only to find an old city
That lived still in that time.
I wanted to find some people,
Souls that I could call mine.
For I’ve not found them,
On this vast wide world.
And I leapt into the sea
To find what hides the sun.




~~~




The towering timbers, uninviting firs.
A home where the ice never melts
And we’re always low on fuel. 
South beckons in the winter cold
To colored trees and warmer beds
I can’t get those words out my head
They cloak me like the morning frost
But fresh waters from fallen snows
Clear my fevered head.
The last light falls from dimming sun
But the warmer winds do not cease
The tea from needles soothes
Eyelids heavy I begin to snooze
It can’t be much longer till
The morning comes to give me fill.




~~~




I walked the earth as a titan would
But could not, as the wind, move the trees.
My father chained heavens to blossom will
He taught me the spirits of liberty.
I cried to the stars that I am a god
But they would not listen to me,
And I wandered the slogs
Of those who are all too many.
The humanists clamored that they alone
Could of all life in the universe be
Superior and intelligent, happy and free.
Their disregard for the flora was sickening.
I lay my riches bare at their feet
And they, disgusted and ignorant, flee.
So I roll up my wares, and I leave them
I took all that they had, and watched them go
For I alone knew the price of my goods
While they thought, better than me, to know.
I saw the heavens in their minds,
But they saw only hell.




~~~




So long, so far, such longing for embrace
Beautiful soul, fair as the blooming trees
Cherished wounding, shining phantom-blushed face
Sweet nectar of Christ who anointed thee.
Soft skin, gentle smell, wrapping me in grace
Your fingers touch delicate smiling lips
Forbidden love, lost moments that I trace,
My hands in dreams pull you close for a kiss.
Can one man be an island, an isthmus?
When I crave the world rest in my grasp.
When words speak false what hearts alone can say
To break this burden and to love at last
I assuredly speak a damning truth
When I say that I am in love with you.




~~~




No amount of words shall ever suffice
To bring your lips any closer to mine
The land alone, and social mores divide
That blissful encounter in wond’ring eyes.
If land be bridged and the fates beguiled
Cause my arms enwrapped in darling wild
I’ll promise you love and walk for miles
With a single kiss worth all the trials.
The mountains raged and the bitter storms cried
And the trees and grasses and flowers sighed
The roads crumbling were swept with autumn snow
My feet grew numb, I began to lose hope 
Swift winter scratched at the doors of my soul
Till in your arms, and warm, I found my home. 




~~~




Let me melt, let me succumb
In the loving arms of my darling one.
How the sun cried foul in the face of night,
While the moon came up to show its light!
The stars filled in when both were gone
To give a show for their darling ones
We’ll sail on through that starry sea
What wonderful signs we two will be!
The worlds all fall away below
And we are left somewhere afloat.
The ocean tastes of berries and rum
All amongst a trillion suns.
Our solitude gives us space to breathe
The wonderful eternal mysteries.


We lay in silence on the old wood raft
While all the others think we’re daft
The silence told me we had begun
To think and see like we were one.
I saw the stars through two pairs of eyes
And I knew that we would never die.
I did melt. Aye, I did succumb,
And so two souls turned into one,
And no greater love was ever known
Than the love we had all of our own.


Let me melt. Let me succumb
Pray let me hold you in the sun.
Let our hearts dance in the leaves 
Of the hanging willow trees.
I do not see the river,
But the river that’s in you
Its flowing sacred waters
Most perfectly imbued. 
We brushed our cheeks, quiet and soft
As the stars through a misty loft–




~~~




If all of me should pass away,
My love for you would ever stay
In smooth waters flowing straight across
Soft mountain stones and river moss.
In city lights and busy streets,
My love would choose to rest its feet.
Its heart I’d lay in oceans deep,
Its mind in meadows of the kings.
My love will rest in every ill,
In every cure and blessing too.
Till you find your heaven here on earth
I will see what I can do.




~~~




I have secrets, dark secrets that I never tell
I keep them hidden, mine alone, the ne’er do well
They’re beautiful, dark, luscious rose petals.
I lay them on my carved stone heart
So solid, so sure, so old and so worn.
My treasure in the forest of unhappy fairy tales
My refuge in times of sadness and morose
My cold stone carved heart that I hold
It’s mine and mine alone.
My beautiful carved heart, my beautiful carved stone.
I carved my secrets there. Ancient Runes.
No one can know the ancient runes.
No one can know but me.
All the beauty in the world, mine alone.
But soon, too soon, will I be gone.
And so too, my beautiful stone.

(I do declare I am a man free
What do I care if others be or not be
I walk upon wings of wind and sit softly in the night
My fears fled me long ago and all in the world is right
How foolish to fret that others live dead
When my worrying gives nought any life to their heads
If I am a god, what a powerless god that would be!
Deaf ears meet my pleas and they continue free
From my influence. What a thought that I’d think
I could change their thoughts with belligerence!
Oh! the wars I fought. How can one man make known
What he was not by another shown? When the truth aches
From longing of the soul, only by love
Pure without hate can one be made whole.)
