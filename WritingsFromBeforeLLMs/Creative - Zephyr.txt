Zephyr


What does one call the soft breeze
And quiet clouds of an early autumn day,
What word does one use to mean
That listless way it feels so strange?
When suddenly you’re another time another place
That you go in only the where-you-are;
Unbridled thoughts wander through the air
To think of books this emotion wrought.
You long for tales by foreign hands,
Different in ways all to your own,
To find brothers and sisters in distant lands
To know you belong with kindred souls,
But cannot think of any book you know,
To dissuade the fact that you are all alone.
