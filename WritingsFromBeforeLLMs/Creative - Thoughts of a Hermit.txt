You wake up one morning, as one normally does the light of the sun burns the sleep from your eyes. You feel the desire to scream and shout and destroy the systems that bind you to this "life". You know it would be pointless because you have learned this and you have fought it in many ways.

World weary is a term you are intensely intimate with it is your second name. Words of professors, teachers, professionals ring in your ears as hollow as you know they had to make themselves fit in certain kind of box and now they live this way. Slaves to the of whims of others can forget that most chains are mental.

When you see nature it sparks life in your body thrumming deep within your heart calling you to the wild spaces. Still nothing fits quite right those who claim freedom, they bind themselves to new masters, new idols and call each other out hoping for more power within their own dynamics and cliches.

This all bothers you and you want to change it yet no one wants to listen to someone who has answers they want to follow. So you need to lead yet leading is dangerous it can be awful, scary and not why you want to be alive. You want someone to stand beside you, challenge you and help you become yourself and simply exist with you.

You want to see the world through eyes that have not yet acquired a tired sense of being alive. Someone who can make the old things new. You want an Idea of a person not the actual thing because life comes with baggage.

Eventually you find yourself receding from life, hiding in your room, online away from the others who just don't get you. They feel uncomfortable with silence they need to talk but you have nothing to say. You need to live to be able to speak of living. The world is always outside, yet you found your inner world and there things are tumultuous but above all they are yours. You are sovereign there your mind can revolt yet still it is yours and no one else can deal with those problems but you.

You need an escape from just being, and need to be alive. This need has been swelling in you for years, your parents told you could be anything yet it did not convince you, you said you wanted be something only to please others. You lived for others, you know the right things to say and now you just want to say the wrong things. You don't want to reply to emails you just want to stop being.

Everything, working, learning, playing, loving, living. Death is a nice idea that draws you in yet it is fear and worry for others well being after you are gone; it is that which holds you back.

So you live fearfully, your sovereignty as being alive is taken, you resign from choosing. You give up. This is not the end, your story is not finished and the pages keep turning whether you want them to or not. You hope there will be a new chapter or the book will end. You need a moment of great change to find reason in living again. Otherwise you are hollow and nothing can satisfy you.

No one truly warned you about how lonely life is and how it is the self that bring about the tendencies to destroy yourself. You find angels and devils littering your life and sometimes they are disguised as each other, it's confusing and you need rules but rules take your freedom. Failure is the only reason we live because in success we find boredom we need the challenge to qualify the existence.

But all the challenges we face appear insurmountable so we give in to retreat and instead find release in consumption of the world. We consume to fill the emptiness of being alive.

We need each other, we need a role in life and none are universal. We need to learn, we need to fight, we need to release, we need to lose. Above that we need to love.
